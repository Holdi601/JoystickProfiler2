Fate of the Galaxy

Prikaži:

Ova stranica još nije prevedena. Prikazuje engleski tekst.

Fate of the Galaxy is a Star Wars space combat game built on FreeSpace Open and started with Knossos.NET. The game keeps your controls in a preset file; your pilot only names the preset it uses. JoyPro reads the preset your pilot uses into your profile and writes your binds as the preset JoyPro_FotG, then tells your pilot to use it. It finds portable Knossos folders on any drive (a kn_portable folder next to Knossos.NET.exe) and the library of an installed Knossos.NET.

Experimental: support for Fate of the Galaxy is new and built from the game's own file formats; if something looks wrong in the game, send JoyPro's support file. The game has no joystick modifiers, so JoyPro never puts its binds behind a JoyPro shift button, and each control takes up to two inputs.

Za početnike

#Pokrenite u 3 koraka

  1. Postavi sve umjesto mene
    na Početnoj
  2. Koristi ove tipke
    prvo pogledajte popis
  3. Zapiši u moju igru
    igra neka ostane zatvorena

Before: start the game once and create your pilot. The game puts your connected devices into its four device slots (Options: Device 1 to Device 4) the first time it starts.

Then: your binds are in the game's controls menu, preset JoyPro_FotG.

#What JoyPro does for Fate of the Galaxy

  • Import: the controls of the preset your pilot uses come into your profile (sticks, throttles, pedals; keys with Also take the keyboard keys).
  • Relations: one relation is one function in every game. "Fire lasers" can be the same trigger in Fate of the Galaxy, Elite Dangerous and X4.
  • Export: JoyPro writes the preset JoyPro_FotG and selects it for your pilot and your campaign saves. Your keys and mouse binds stay.
  • Auto setup: lasers, missiles, weapon banks, targeting, match speed, energy, shields and views go on your stick and throttle the way a space fighter pilot needs them.

#Export to Fate of the Galaxy

  1. Close the game. It writes your pilot when it closes.
  2. Open Export and tick the Fate of the Galaxy card.
  3. Ostavite Zamijeni na mojim uređajima s opcijom Očisti i moje druge uređaje ako palice postavljate u JoyPro. Pritisnite Izvezi sada na kartici.
U igri: Što vidite u igri

Start the game and open the controls menu: the preset is JoyPro_FotG and your binds are on Joy-0 to Joy-3, the four devices of the options.

#What you do yourself in the game

  • Create a pilot first. Without one JoyPro writes the preset only; then pick JoyPro_FotG in the controls menu (or export again after creating the pilot).
  • Keep your devices in Device 1-4. The game reads four devices. A device without a slot takes a free one; with all four taken, choose it in the game's options and export again.

#Česte zamke

  • **"The game is running"**: exit the game and export again.
  • A bind was skipped: the game reads 128 buttons, the first hat in four directions (no diagonals) and eight axes per device, and two inputs per control; JoyPro names every bind it could not write.
  • Afterburner, countermeasures or sliding thrust are missing: the mod switches them off; JoyPro shows only the controls the game's menu has.
  • You changed binds in the game: the game saves them as a new preset you name. JoyPro's watcher notices it after the game closes and asks whether to take them over.
Napredno

#Datoteke koje JoyPro čita i zapisuje

Datoteka Što je to
kn_portable\HardLightProductions\FreeSpaceOpen\ (portable) or %APPDATA%\HardLightProductions\FreeSpaceOpen\ the game's user data
...\data\players\presets\JoyPro_FotG.json the preset JoyPro writes: every control with a primary and a secondary input
...\data\players\<pilot>.json your pilot; JoyPro changes only the name of its preset (and the dead zone / sensitivity you set)
...\data\players\<pilot>.<campaign>.csg the campaign saves; they name the preset too
...\data\FotG_2_settings.ini the four device slots ([Input] Joystick ... Joystick3), dead zone and sensitivity
<Knossos library>\FotG_2\FotG_2-<version>\ the mod: its control table and texts, read only for the command list

Every file gets a .jpbak copy and a versioned backup before the first write.

#The export modes in Fate of the Galaxy

Način Što se događa u predlošku
Samo dodaj Nothing is removed. A bind whose input another control has is not written and gets a mark.
Ažuriraj, zadrži dodjele igre Your binds go into a free place of the control; only conflicts are removed.
Ažuriraj, moj profil ima prednost As above, and a control you bind loses its other joystick inputs (with the keyboard option also its keys).
Zamijeni na mojim uređajima The devices you export keep only your binds. Keys and mouse binds stay.
+ Očisti i moje druge uređaje The same for the other connected devices of Device 1-4.

#Krivulje i postavke igre

  • Invert per bind. A throttle lever pushed forward gives more thrust without an invert (the game reads the axis the same way as DCS); JoyPro writes the game's invert flag only when you invert the axis yourself.
  • JoyPro's notes for a re-import sit next to your pilot as <pilot>.json.joypro-meta, a name the game ignores. JoyPro 2.7.3 used <pilot>.json.joypro-meta.json, which the game tried to load as a pilot and then stopped at start; the next export removes that old file (or delete it in data\players yourself).
  • Dead zone (0-45) and sensitivity (0-9) are one setting for every axis: set them in Settings > Force feedback settings of the games (pick Fate of the Galaxy) or with engine ffb set. The axis curve preview shows the game's response with them.
  • Watcher: your pilot and the mod's settings are watched; JoyPro asks after the game closed.
Tehnički detalji

#Inside the integration

Code: engine\Games\FreeSpaceOpenIntegration.cs and engine\Games\FreeSpaceOpen\ (control list, files, Knossos discovery), the axis model engine\AxisModels\FreeSpaceOpen.cs and the settings engine\FfbModels\FreeSpaceOpen.cs. The formats follow FreeSpace Open 26.0.1's own source. Other FreeSpace Open mods could use the same backend later.

  • Inputs: CID_JOY0 .. CID_JOY3 are Device 1-4; buttons 0 .. 127, the first hat 128 back, 129 forward, 130 left, 131 right; axes X_AXIS .. V_AXIS count over the axes your device has.
  • Command list: engine fotg refresh-db reads the mod's controlconfigdefaults.tbl and its English texts, read only. engine fotg catalog lists it.
  • Import / export: engine fotg import, engine fotg scan; engine fotg export --mode clean|add|merge|merge-overwrite --nuke-untouched-devices --include-keyboard --dry-run.

#Poznata ograničenja

  • Not checked in the running game yet; the formats come from the game engine's source and real files of an installation.
  • Two identical sticks are told apart by the device number the game saved.
  • Tests: engine\tests\smoke\fotg.ps1.
