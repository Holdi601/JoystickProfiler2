GUI pages

Profile bar

Show:

The profile bar sits above every page. It names the open profile and holds what works everywhere: open or start a profile, the save state, Back and Forward, Undo and Redo, the profile's history, the More menu and the expert Options. The bottom bar below every page switches between the pages. A notice under the bar tells you when devices were moved to a new ID.

For beginners

#What is a profile?

A profile is one file (.pr0file) with everything you set up in JoyPro: your relations, which button does what (binds), shift buttons (modifiers) and axis curves. You can have several profiles, but one is open at a time. Its name is on the left of the bar; hover it to see where the file is.

#The buttons from left to right

  • Open profile...: pick a .pr0file file.
  • New profile: an empty profile. It gets a name with the date in Documents\JoyPro2\profiles; More > Rename... gives it yours. Easier: import a game on the Start page, which makes a profile for you.
  • Saved ✓: JoyPro saves every change by itself. While it saves you see Saving…; when a save fails, Not saved with the reason (it tries again).
  • Back / Forward (arrows): go to where you were before, like a browser (page, open relation, search, scroll position). They never change the profile.
  • Undo / Redo: take back the last change of the profile, or do it again (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y). The tooltip names the change ("Undo Adding relation").
  • History: every saved version of the profile, to look at, compare, restore or keep.
  • More: open the last profile, save a copy, rename, add another profile, reload, close, check for updates, about.
  • Options: settings for experts (paths and how a profile is repaired when it loads).

#The bottom bar

One button per page: Start, Relations, Binds, Modifiers, Layout, Export, HUD, Force Feedback, Virtual Joystick, Statistics, Cockpits, Settings, Advanced, plus Check and the Output panel. Settings > Pages in the bottom bar hides pages you do not use and changes their order.

Advanced

#Undo, History and Back are three different things

What it walks through Changes the profile?
Back / Forward the places you were: page, sub-view, open relation or device, search texts, filters, scroll position (at most 50) no
Undo / Redo the profile's versions, one step at a time, also after a restart yes
History every version with what it changed; restore any of them yes (a restore is a new version, so Undo goes back)

Back and Forward also work with Alt+Left / Alt+Right and the mouse's back and forward buttons.

#The History panel

  • Left: the versions, newest first ("Adding relation", "Import from DCS", "Before export to DCS"), with now on the version the profile is at and kept on versions you keep.
  • Details shows what a version changed ("Relation 'Gear' renamed to 'Landing gear'"); Compare shows what differs between that version and the profile now.
  • Keep protects a version from the clean-up; Restore makes the profile as it was then.
  • Clean up thins out old versions of all profiles now: all of the last day, then fewer per hour, day and week. JoyPro also does this by itself in the background. Kept versions always stay.

#Device notice

When you open a profile and a device has a new ID (Windows gives devices new IDs now and then, or a new stick of the same model), JoyPro moves its binds and shows a notice under the bar: which device moved where. Undo puts the binds back on the old device, Replace device... opens the window to move binds yourself, input by input, OK closes the notice.

#Replace device window

Replace device... (the notice, a device card on the Layout page, Settings > Devices) moves the binds of an old device to another one:

  • Automatic: the same product gets the same input ids; another device is matched by its parts.
  • Same input ids (1:1): button 5 stays button 5, X stays X.
  • By layout (device catalog): trigger to trigger, hat 2 to hat 2, throttle to throttle; buttons without a matching part go by their order (shown as a guess).
  • When the new device already has binds on some inputs: Keep both, Keep the new device's binds or Replace them with the old device's binds.
  • Move binds asks first, keeps a copy of the profile next to it and does it as shown (Undo takes it back); Cancel closes without a change.
In the game: moved binds

Moving binds to another device changes nothing in a game until the next export; then the binds are written for the new device. With Replace on my devices the old device's binds are cleared in the exported aircraft.

Technical internals

#Behind the bar

  • Profile commands: MainWindowViewModel.cs (OpenLastProfileAsync, LoadProfileAsync, MergeProfileAsync, SaveLastProfileAsync, ClearWorkspace, RefreshAllAsync), the menu's file dialogs in Views/MainWindow.axaml.cs.
  • Autosave and history: Services/AutosaveScheduler.cs, MainWindowViewModel.ProfileHistory.cs, the store engine/ProfileHistory.cs in %LOCALAPPDATA%\JoyPro2\ProfileHistory\<profile id>\ (history.json, one gzip blob per distinct content, drafts.json). Details: profile-history.
  • Back / Forward: Services/NavigationHistory.cs, MainWindowViewModel.NavigationHistory.cs, Views/MainWindow.Navigation.cs (ui/navigation).
  • Device notice and Replace device: MainWindowViewModel.DeviceReplace.cs, Views/ReplaceDeviceWindow.axaml.
  • Updates: MainWindowViewModel.Updates.cs (updates).

Every GUI action runs engine.exe with --profile <file>, --status, --benchmark and the load-correction options of Options (--load-corrections, --auto-add-db-items, --remap-missing-devices, --correction-mode, --correction-auto-threshold). Engine: engine profile load, engine profile merge, engine profile save-last, engine profile summary, engine profile history, engine profile history show, engine profile history restore, engine profile history keep, engine profile history prune, engine devices replace, engine devices auto-remap.

Goes back to the place you were before: the page, its tab or panel, the open relation or device, search texts, filters and scroll positions (also Alt+Left and the mouse's back button). The profile is not changed; the tooltip names where it goes.

Goes forward again after Back (Alt+Right, the mouse's forward button). A new place you open clears Forward.

Opens the profile you had open last (its path is the tooltip). Pending edits of the current profile are saved first. Engine: engine profile load.

Closes the Profile and engine options panel (the Options button opens it again).

Merges the Merge profile file into the open profile: relations, binds and modifiers are added; where both have the same relation, the setting above decides which one wins. Asks first and keeps a copy of your profile next to it. Engine: engine profile merge <yours> <other> --mode left|right --save <yours>.

On (default): an import or another change while no profile is open makes a new profile for it in Documents\JoyPro2\profiles. Off: such commands stop with "Profile path required".

On (default): every engine command repairs the profile while it loads (--load-corrections true): actions whose game command changed with a game update are matched to the current command, and missing devices are looked for. Off: the profile is read exactly as stored; the two boxes next to it are then off too.

With load corrections: when an action is matched to a corrected command of the command list, that command is added to the relation in its place (--auto-add-db-items). How sure a match must be: Correction Mode (strict, balanced, aggressive) and Auto Threshold below; Correction Report writes what was changed to a file.

With load corrections: binds of a device that is not connected are moved to a connected device of the same model with a new ID (--remap-missing-devices). The device notice under the bar lists what moved and offers Undo.

Keeps the engine's status and progress lines in the Output panel (--status true). Off: the busy area still shows them while a command runs, but the Output keeps only the result.

Adds the engine's timings to every command's output (--benchmark true): how long loading, the command and saving took. For finding slow steps.

Closes the History panel (the History button opens it again).

Closes the notice about moved devices. The moved binds stay; Undo is then only possible through the History panel.

Closes the Replace device window without moving anything.

Light in the banner at the top: shows JoyPro in its light colours at once. The same choice as in Settings > Appearance; kept for the next start.

Dark in the banner at the top: shows JoyPro in its dark colours at once. Kept for the next start.

System in the banner at the top: JoyPro follows the Windows setting for apps (light or dark). Kept for the next start.

Picks a JoyPro profile (.pr0file) and opens it. Pending edits of the profile that is open now are saved first, so nothing is lost. Engine: engine profile load.

Starts an empty profile at once, without a dialog: it gets a name with the date in Documents\JoyPro2\profiles (More > Rename... gives it yours). Pending edits of the open profile are saved first. Easier for a first profile: import a game on the Start page, which makes one for you. Engine: engine profile new.

Takes back the last change of the profile (Ctrl+Z); the tooltip names it ("Undo Adding relation"). Edits not saved yet are saved first, so they are undone first. Works across restarts, one version of the History at a time. Your games change only with the next export.

Does the change you just undid again (Ctrl+Y); the tooltip names it. Enabled only when there is an undone change to redo.

Opens or closes the History panel below the bar: every saved version of this profile, newest first, with Details, Compare, Keep and Restore. Only with a profile open. Opening it changes nothing.

Opens the menu of the less used profile actions: Open last profile, Save as..., Rename..., Add another profile..., Reload, Close profile, Check for updates and About JoyPro.

More > Save as...: saves a copy of the open profile under a name and folder you choose; from then on JoyPro works on the copy and remembers it as the last profile. The original file stays as it was. Engine: engine profile save.

More > Rename...: gives the profile file another name or folder; its history goes along. When a profile with that name exists, JoyPro asks first and keeps the replaced file as <name>.pr0file.bak next to it, with its history. A file another program holds open stops the rename and nothing changes.

More > Add another profile...: picks another .pr0file and merges its relations, binds and modifiers into the open profile. Asks first and keeps a copy of your profile next to it; where both have the same relation, Options decides which one wins. Needs an open profile. Engine: engine profile merge.

More > Reload: reads the profile summary, the relations, binds and modifiers, the devices and the DCS aircraft list again, for example after you changed the profile file outside JoyPro. Changes nothing. Engine: engine profile summary.

More > Close profile: saves pending edits and closes the profile in JoyPro, so every page is empty. The file and its history stay; Open profile... or More > Open last profile opens it again.

More > Check for updates: asks GitHub for the latest release of JoyPro and always says what it found. A newer version is offered with what changed (Update now, Later, Skip this version); nothing is downloaded without your click.

More > About JoyPro and the version at the bottom right: opens Settings > About JoyPro and updates (version, build, licence line, updates). When a newer version is on offer, the version corner names it.

Options: opens or closes the Profile and engine options panel below the bar, for experts: what wins when you add another profile, the engine and profile paths, and how a profile is repaired when it loads. Normal use needs none of it.

Options panel: saves the open profile as the engine's own last profile (last.pr0file), the one engine commands use when no profile is given. Your profile file is not changed. Engine: engine profile save-last.

History panel: thins out the old versions of all profiles now: all of the last day, then one per hour for 7 days, one per day for 60 days, one per week after that. Kept versions always stay. JoyPro also does this by itself a minute after start and every 6 hours; the status line says how much space it freed.

Details on a version: lists on the right what this version changed against the one before it ("Relation 'Gear' renamed to 'Landing gear'"). Changes nothing.

Compare on a version: lists on the right everything that differs between that version and the profile as it is now. Changes nothing.

Keep on a version: a kept version is marked kept and never cleaned up. Click it again to let the clean-up remove it like any other version.

Restore on a version: asks first, then makes the profile as it was then. The restore is a new version, so the current state stays in the History and Undo (Ctrl+Z) brings it back. In the game: the restored binds arrive with the next export.

Closes the list of Details or Compare on the right of the History panel.

Replace device... in the notice about moved devices: opens the window to move the binds of a device to another one yourself, input by input, with a preview, for example a new stick or a device JoyPro did not move by itself.

Undo in the notice about moved devices: puts the binds back on the old devices and closes the notice. Works while the move is still the last change; after other changes JoyPro opens the History panel instead, where you restore the version before the move.

Cancel next to the progress bar at the bottom: stops a longer command before it writes anything. JoyPro offers it only while the command still reads; the button says "Cancelling..." until it has stopped.

Opens the Start page: your games with import, export, update and undo, the game watcher and the Setup wizard.

Opens the Relations page: which game commands belong together, so one button does the same thing in every game and aircraft.

Opens the Binds page: which button or axis of your devices does what, with recording and axis curves.

Opens the Modifiers page: shift buttons, held down so the other buttons get a second job.

Opens the Layout page: pictures of your devices with what every button does.

Opens the Export page: write your binds into the games, the check, undo and the game config backups. Pressed while the page is open, it reads the backup list again (engine backups list).

Opens the HUD page: the designer for an on-screen or streaming view of your sticks, throttles and pedals live.

Opens the Force Feedback page: force feedback from the game's telemetry, its settings, the graph editor and the telemetry inspector.

Opens the Virtual Joystick page: per game one virtual vJoy joystick or Xbox pad instead of your real devices, with HidHide and virtual functions.

Opens the Statistics page: your most played games, aircraft and ships, streaks and fun facts from your games' logs, on this PC only. Shown only when Settings > Usage statistics > Show the Statistics page in the bottom bar is on.

Opens the Cockpits page: stick and throttle of real and fictional aircraft per version, with JoyPro's drawings, the panels and sources.

Opens the Settings page: games and their folders, devices, command lists, force feedback, appearance, updates and problem reports. It cannot be hidden from the bar.

Opens the Advanced page, for experts: type engine commands yourself and see their output.

Show it always, in the hint next to the bottom bar while a page you hid in Settings > Pages in the bottom bar is open: shows that page in the bar for good again. Kept for the next start.

Replace device window, default: the same product gets the same input ids; another device is matched by its parts (trigger to trigger, hat to hat). The preview below updates at once.

Replace device window: button 5 stays button 5, X stays X. For the same product, or a device you set up the same way. The preview updates at once.

Replace device window: matches by the device catalog: trigger to trigger, hat 2 to hat 2, roll to roll, throttle to throttle. Buttons without a matching part go by their order and are marked as a guess in the preview.

Shown when the new device already has binds on some of the target inputs, default: both binds stay on that input. Fine when they are for different games or aircraft.

The new device keeps its own binds on those inputs; the old device's binds for them stay on the old device.

The new device's binds on those inputs are removed and the old device's binds take their place. Undo brings them back.

Move binds: asks first, keeps a copy of the profile next to it (<profile>.before-device-replace-<time>.pr0file) and moves every bind and modifier of the old device to the new one as the preview shows, as one change, and closes the window. The notice under the bar says how many moved and which stayed on the old device; its Undo (or Ctrl+Z) takes it back. Engine: engine devices replace. In the game: the binds are on the new device after the next export.
