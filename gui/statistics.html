GUI pages

Statistics

Show:

The Statistics page shows what you play and fly, across all your games: hours per game, your top 10 aircraft, ships and vehicles, when you play, fun facts, what the games counted (landings, kills, jumps) and how your profile grew. The numbers come from the games' own logs, journals, logbooks and replays and from Steam's playtime. Everything stays on your PC.

The page is off by default: switch it on in Settings > Usage statistics > Show the Statistics page in the bottom bar.

For beginners

#What is on the page

  • Tiles at the top: hours played in total (and how many of them your games logged themselves), sessions, the most played game, the longest streak.
  • Hours per game: one bar per game, from the game's own log or Steam's playtime, whichever is higher.
  • Top 10 aircraft, ships and vehicles: only games that log what you fly (DCS, IL-2, X-Plane, Falcon BMS, Elite Dangerous, Star Citizen, Tacview).
  • When you play: hours per day of the week, sessions started per hour of the day, hours per month.
  • Fun facts: "You flew the F-16C more than all helicopters together", your longest session, your first recorded flight, light years jumped. More facts come with more recorded sessions.
  • Counted in your games: landings, kills, deaths, jumps and distance, where the game logs them.
  • Your profile: binds per device, the oldest bind you have not used, how your relations and binds grew over the profile's history, exports per game.
  • Where the numbers come from: each source and what it gave.

Nothing recorded yet? Play a game JoyPro supports: JoyPro reads its log when the game closes (and at the next start of JoyPro). Steam's playtime counts as soon as Steam has some.

Advanced

#Details

  • Read the games' logs now reads what the games wrote since the last time and redraws the page; normally this happens by itself at start and after a game closes.
  • Nothing counts twice: a session read again (the log grew, moved into a backup folder, the live copy) replaces its record, the more complete read wins.
  • Some games log nothing about the aircraft: MSFS keeps its logbook online (Steam's playtime only), War Thunder replays give the battle length but no vehicle. IL-2 writes mission reports only with mission_text_log = 1 in its data\startup.cfg; JoyPro says so and never changes the game's files.
  • The same numbers drive the ranking in Settings > Usage statistics: pin an aircraft there to make it the model for new aircraft of its class, or exclude one that should not count.
  • Like every page, Statistics can be moved or hidden in Settings > Pages in the bottom bar.
Technical internals

#Behind the page

View model StatisticsViewModel.cs (its own view model; the main window passes the engine calls), view Views/StatisticsView.axaml, charts Views/Controls/UsageCharts.cs; the harvest and ranking in MainWindowViewModel.Usage.cs. Engine: engine usage harvest reads new log data into <data>\Usage (sessions.jsonl, totals.json, seen.json, exports.jsonl, overrides.json), engine usage stats returns the whole page as one JSON document (with --profile also binds per device, growth and the oldest unused bind), engine usage sources lists what exists on this PC. Every file is opened read-only and shared, so a game that writes its log is never disturbed. Reference: Usage statistics.

Reads what your games logged since the last time (logs, journals, logbooks, replays, Steam's playtime) and redraws the page. Normally JoyPro does this by itself at start and after a game closes. Engine: engine usage harvest, then engine usage stats. Changes nothing in your games or your profile.
