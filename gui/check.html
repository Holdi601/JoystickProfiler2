Check

Show:

Check in the bottom bar runs the profile check and shows the results on the Export page under Problems found by the check. What each kind of card means and how the fixes work: The Check.

▶
Video: The Check page

For beginners

#Using the list

  • The line at the top counts the cards: "To look at: 0 errors, 1 warning, 0 notes".
  • Search finds cards by relation, aircraft, button and command.
  • The first box shows one kind (All problems, errors, warnings, notes); the second orders them (By relation, By aircraft, By game, By confidence).
  • Expand all / Collapse all open or close every section.
  • A card's blue button with a tick is JoyPro's suggestion; More has the other fixes; Open '' jumps to the relation with the card's commands marked.
  • Ignore on a card keeps the binds as they are and hides the warning. It comes back by itself, marked New and "Shown again: now also on ...", when the command lands on one more button. So you work through the list once and afterwards see only what is new.
  • Ignored warnings (n) (only while you ignored some) lists the hidden ones with Show again for each and Show all again. Typing "ignore list", "hidden" or "muted" into the search opens it too.
Advanced

#Fixing many at once

Fix all with a rule applies one rule to every card of a section, Fix with AI takes an instruction in plain words; both show a Preview with a tick per change, Apply the ticked ones applies them as one undo step. Also right after imports and take-overs runs the sure fixes automatically after an import.

Closes every section, so only the section titles with their counts show.

"Ignored warnings (n)": opens or closes the list of the warnings you ignored with Ignore on their card. It shows only while there are some. Each one is described with the card's words (the buttons with their relations, the aircraft and the command) and has Show again, which puts the card back on the check (one Ctrl+Z takes it back). The search above filters the list as well; searching for "ignore list", "hidden warnings", "dismissed" or "muted" opens it. An ignored warning also comes back on its own when one more button, relation or command joins it: the card then says "Shown again: now also on ..." and is marked New. The other fixes of the card: switch it off on both buttons, move to another button, remove an unknown command, find similar commands.

Opens the box Fix with AI (and closes Fix all with a rule): describe in a sentence where the actions of "actions on in several relations" belong, for example "keep trim functions in the Trim relations, everything else in the Mode Button relations". JoyPro reads simple sentences itself; the optional local AI only helps with the rest. Nothing changes until you preview and apply.

Shows what the chosen rule would change (a rule such as "keep it on the device ..." or "keep it in the relations named ..."), as a list of ticked lines "F-16C 'Trim Up': keep in 'Trim', off in 'Hat Up'" with how sure JoyPro is. Changes nothing. Engine: engine relations duplicates --rule.

Shows what your sentence would change, with "Understood: ..." and "Not understood: ..." lines so you see how JoyPro read it. Changes nothing. Engine: engine relations duplicates --instruction ... --llm auto (the local AI is used only when it is switched on in Settings > Local AI).

One change of the preview. Untick the lines you do not want; Apply the ticked ones applies only the ticked lines.

Ticks every line of the preview, or unticks all of them when all are ticked already.

Closes the preview without changing anything.

A fix of a card (the blue one with the tick is JoyPro's suggestion) or an Open '' button. A fix runs one engine command, so it is one undo step (Ctrl+Z). Open shows the relation with exactly the card's commands and aircraft marked; Back to the check above the relation's name (or Back, Alt+Left) returns to the card. In the game: the fix takes effect at the next export.

Ignore (on "Same command on two buttons", "One button does two things" and "One button runs two commands"; it was called "Keep both" before 2.6.1) changes no bind: the card goes away and JoyPro remembers it in the profile. It comes back when the command lands on one more button (one more relation on the button, one more command in the relation), marked "Shown again: now also on ...". In the list Ignored warnings, Show again puts one card back.
