GUI pages

Setup wizard

Show:

The Setup wizard sets up your controls for your games in one go. It looks at your connected devices, gives each one a role (main stick, throttle, pedals, panel ...), makes relations for the important functions of your games' aircraft, binds them to your devices and shows you the result as cheat sheets. You review everything before your profile changes, and one Undo takes it all back. It runs without AI: the same choices always give the same plan.

Open it from the Start page. Its card "Set up your devices" has two buttons:

  • Set it all up for me (the blue one): the quick setup. It runs this wizard by itself, without showing the steps, and stops at a plain list of what goes where. See Quick start.
  • Set up step by step...: this wizard with all five steps, so you choose each one yourself.

Without that card (every game is set up already), press Set up my devices automatically... under Your games on the Start page, or on the Binds page.

  1. Games
    which games to set up
  2. Devices
    what each device is for
  3. Priorities
    your aircraft, modifiers, where functions go
  4. Plan
    what JoyPro placed
  5. Review
    check, then Apply to my profile

The quick setup (Set it all up for me) runs the same planner without these steps. It uses your devices' usual roles, every aircraft equal, no modifier and your last choice of Where should functions go?. For a flight sim it asks one question: Do you use head tracking or VR?

For beginners

#The five steps

The steps are shown at the top; Back and Next at the bottom move between them. Close the window at any time: a wizard you close before Apply comes back as you left it.

#1. Games

The games you watch on the Start page are ticked. Tick the games this setup should cover. This does not change which games JoyPro watches.

#2. Devices

JoyPro recognises your sticks, throttles, pedals and panels and proposes a role for each one: "What this device is for". Change a role in its list. One device per hand: a second stick for the same hand gets "unused" unless you choose otherwise. A base that takes several grips asks for the grip. The box on the right says in words how JoyPro will use them. Detect again reads the devices again (plug in a device first).

#3. Priorities

  • Your aircraft: search an aircraft ("F-16", "helicopter", "Star Citizen") and press Add as next priority (a new row) or Add as equal (into the selected row). The higher an aircraft, the better places its functions get. Aircraft in one row count the same; aircraft you do not list count least.
  • Modifiers: a modifier is a button you hold (like Shift) so every other button does a second thing. Choose how many; As few as possible uses one only when your buttons run out.
  • Mode switch (phase layers): a latched switch can change what your buttons do per combat phase.
  • Where should functions go?: a slider. Left end: Same button for the same job in every aircraft. Right end: Where the real aircraft has it. The middle is the start. The gun and the throttle never move. See Where should functions go?.
  • Do you use head tracking or VR? Only for flight sims. Yes if you look around by moving your head (TrackIR, a face tracking webcam, Tobii or VR), or plan to: no look buttons on your devices, only "recenter the view". No: looking around goes on a hat of your stick or throttle, right after the most important buttons. See Head tracking and looking around.

#4. Plan

JoyPro makes the plan: how many functions it placed, what it could not place, the modifiers it made, the rules it followed and a quality score per aircraft (100 % = every function on its best place).

#5. Review

Pick a Device and an Aircraft to see JoyPro's drawing of the device with what is bound where. Select a function in the list to move it to a free input, swap it with another one or take it out of the plan. Not placed lists what did not fit, most important first.

When you like it: Apply to my profile, then Export to my games (the Export page opens). Your games change only with the export.

Advanced

#Details

  • Roles: main stick (right hand in every aircraft), heli stick (right hand in helicopters, for a second stick such as a force feedback base), left stick (left hand in space games; the throttle side without a throttle), throttle, collective (left hand in helicopters), pedals (rudder and toe brakes), panel (system switches whose printed label fits: "LDG GEAR UP" gets the gear), keyboard joystick and virtual (kept, nothing new bound), unused.
  • Your profile first: a command that is already in one of your relations stays there, and a relation already bound on a device of this setup keeps its input. A command you switched off stays off.
  • Where should functions go? The slider runs from Same button for the same job in every aircraft to Where the real aircraft has it. Below the middle there is one relation per function for all aircraft; from the middle on, aircraft whose real HOTAS has the function elsewhere get their own relation, and the further right, the stronger the real position pulls. The quick setup and the Start page use your last choice (the middle at first).
  • The choices of step 3 are remembered; the next wizard starts with them.
  • Apply writes everything as one change: one version in the profile History named "Setup wizard: N relations, M binds". Undo (Ctrl+Z) takes all of it back.
  • Open the sheet shows the cheat sheet full size in your picture viewer, to print it.
  • The new layout follows your most used aircraft of its class when Settings > Usage statistics > Lay out new aircraft like my most used one is on.
In the game: After Apply and an export

Nothing until you export. After Apply and an export, each game's control files hold the wizard's binds: in DCS every chosen aircraft has the same function on the same button when the slider is near Same button for the same job in every aircraft, Star Citizen gets the ship controls on the stick and the strafe axes on a left stick, panels get the systems their labels name. Export with Replace on my devices if the devices should lose the binds the game had before.

Technical internals

#Behind the wizard

Window Views/SetupWizardWindow.axaml, view model ViewModels/SetupWizardViewModel.cs (its own view model, created by MainWindowViewModel.SetupWizard.cs with the watched games), planner engine/SetupWizard/*.cs. Every step is an engine command, so the CLI makes the same plan: engine setup devices (recognition and roles, --role, --grip), engine setup aircraft (the priority list), engine setup plan (--games, --priorities "A>B|C", --modifiers, --prefer-fewer or --use-all-modifiers, --placement (old name --realism), --phase-layers, cockpit entries of engine cockpits as --cockpit-file; writes the plan JSON), engine setup render (a callout sheet per device), engine setup apply (--plan, one profile version). Move, swap, remove and put back edit the plan in the GUI (SetupPlanEdit) and are checked again before Apply. Reference: Setup wizard.

Optionwritten2 ×

Your answer is kept; change it later in Settings > Looking around.
