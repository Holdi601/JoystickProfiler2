Relations

Show:

The Relations page builds and organizes relations: one function with its action in every game and aircraft. The walk-through with diagrams and the in-game meaning is on Build relations.

▶
Video: The Relations page
Coming later: one YouTube video per topic, recorded with the English interface, with subtitles in every language JoyPro speaks.
For beginners

#Three areas

  • Left: your relations. A search box, filters (groups, games, devices, bound or not, axis or button) and one card per relation with its button ("Throttle - HOTAS Warthog · BTN 22 · Shift") or "not bound".
  • Middle: the open relation. Its name (click to rename), Axis or Button, its input, and Its actions: one card per game action.
  • Right: tools. Add actions (search every game), Suggested for this relation, Catalog (browse a game's actions by category) and the JoyPro assistant (type a sentence).

New starts a relation; Auto-build relations from all my actions builds them for the whole profile.

Advanced

#Tips

  • Ctrl/Shift+click several cards for Bulk changes (groups, duplicate, delete) and Groups (add, remove, rename).
  • Follow input: press a button on your stick and its relation opens.
  • A DCS action card opens its aircraft ("82 of 84 aircraft"); untick aircraft where the command should not apply. Only same meaning keeps only aircraft where the id means the same.
  • Suggest missing (n) on the open relation lists what other games and aircraft lack; More > Suggest missing for every relation... does all relations at once.
  • The function chip says which function JoyPro thinks the relation is; change it if the guess is wrong (it steers the suggestions).
