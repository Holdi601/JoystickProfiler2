# JoyPro 2.4.0

## New
- **Your own look** (Settings > Appearance): make your own colour themes with a live preview, a contrast check that
  fixes hard-to-read colours for you, and import/export to share them. Pick the font, and make all text bigger or
  smaller on top of the Windows scaling. Pages scroll instead of cutting text at big sizes.
- **Back and Forward** next to Undo: jump back to the page, tab, relation, search and scroll position you came from
  (for example Check, then "Open both relations", then Back).
- **Cheat sheet style** on the Layout page: presets and your own presets, font, text and table colours, markers,
  modifier list and drawing; a device can keep its own style.
- **Cockpits**: pictures taken from the games themselves for almost every aircraft, with every panel readable;
  overhead panels now read the right way round. Aircraft that one game lacks use the pictures of the same aircraft
  from another game. The picture packs download from within JoyPro.
- **Reference photos of your devices**: for a connected device JoyPro offers the maker's photos (Download, Not now,
  Always download, No). They are checked and kept on your PC; the setting is in Settings > Privacy. Better and more
  photos for many devices, and fixed photo links reach you without waiting for a new version.
- **Search scope** on the Relations and Binds pages: names only, names and action titles (the default), or
  everything. Exact names come first.
- DCS import options **Treat devices with the same name as one device** (on by default) and **Only import connected
  devices** (off by default: devices that are not plugged in are imported too). Both are remembered.

## Fixed
- DCS import: a device that Windows gave a new ID over time no longer turns every bind into its own relation. Its
  binds are read as one device, on the connected ID (else the newest one); for each aircraft only the copy DCS really
  uses is read. Two identical devices plugged in at the same time stay apart.
- Check: relations copied like that can be merged back into one ("Copied relations" on the Export page), with every
  aircraft, the groups and the tuning per aircraft. One step to undo.
- Renaming a profile to the name of an existing one did nothing. JoyPro now asks, replaces it and keeps the old file
  as a backup with its history.
- The Relations search also found relations only because of their device name. It now looks at names and action
  titles unless you choose "Everything"; after a search the list starts at its first row.
- Windows such as the support file window could be taller than the screen. All dialogs now fit the screen at any
  scaling, scroll their content and keep their buttons visible.
- JoyPro keeps its data in one folder, %LOCALAPPDATA%\JoyPro2. The old %LOCALAPPDATA%\JoyPro is moved there on the
  first start.
- Layout page: the device cards no longer overlap, and the picture match never shows more than 100 %.
- "Profile path required for this command" no longer appears when no profile is open.
- Some devices showed a photo of another device (for example the Honeycomb throttle quadrant); those photos are gone.
