# JoyPro 2.2.0

Clean drawings of your devices with every button in the right place, 3D views you can turn, and suggestions that
recommend the right action in every game.

## New
- **Device drawings:** clean drawings of your devices with every button, hat (with its directions), switch and axis
  at its real place; every table of the cheat sheets is connected to its control.
- **3D views of devices:** for devices with a 3D model, the Layout page shows a view you can turn with the mouse;
  markers, live highlights and binding follow the rotation. JoyPro offers the download when it finds such a device.
- **Bind by drag and drop:** drag a relation onto a control on the device picture to bind it.
- **Correct a device yourself:** the Layout page tells you which inputs have no place on the picture yet. Press them
  to place them, add missing controls (buttons, hats, mini-sticks, encoders, axes) and send the correction to JoyPro
  with one click.
- **Recommended actions:** suggestions and search mark the main action of each game for a function (for example
  "Elevator Axis" for pitch in Microsoft Flight Simulator) and explain how the other variants differ (alternate set,
  landing mode, helicopter, trim and so on).
- **Readable names and explanations** for the actions of every game, including the names Microsoft Flight Simulator
  uses in its own controls menu.

## Fixed
- Opening another relation could show the suggestions of the relation opened before.
- DCS World: after closing the game, JoyPro could report a bind as removed although it was still active as one of
  the device's default binds.
- Tooltips no longer pop up after a button press or axis movement: they appear only after you move the mouse and
  rest it on an item, and only while JoyPro is the active window.
