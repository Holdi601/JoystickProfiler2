# JoyPro 2.2.15

Clearer relation editor, sorting in levels, and a look into a relation from the Binds page.

## New
- **Sort in levels** (Relations and Binds): sort by device, then input, then name - or any other order of name,
  device, input, axis/button, games, aircraft count, group, bound and modifiers, each A to Z or Z to A. Inputs sort
  naturally (BTN 2 before BTN 10). Each list remembers its sorting.
- **What a bind does** (Binds page): the selected bind shows its relation's actions per game with their aircraft, and
  "Open in Relations".
- **Reference photo** (Layout page): a line drawing made from the maker's photo offers to download that photo (after
  you said yes for the device) and shows it as its own picture.

## Changed
- The note about commands that mean something else in some aircraft is now a plain info with a real example, counts
  only the open relation, and offers "Only where it is ...", "Keep for all" and "Ask me later". The whole profile is
  checked from its own link.
- "Group all my actions automatically" is now "Auto-build relations from all my actions" and says that it opens a
  wizard in place of the relation, with "Close and back to my relation" inside.
- Tools opened at the top of the Relations page close when you leave the page.
- Every action card in the relation editor folds with its arrow; collapsible sections fold from their arrow and the
  whole header row.
- Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024: a profile all your aircraft use is shown as "all your aircraft" instead of a list.
