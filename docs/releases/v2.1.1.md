# JoyPro 2.1.1

Fixes for the reports from the first testers, and a cockpit guide that explains every control.

## New
- **Cockpit guide:** the Cockpits page now has a tree of every area, panel and control of an aircraft. Each panel and
  control has a plain-language explanation of what it does and why it matters, every switch position explained.
  Click a control to see it on the picture, or a callout to find it in the tree; search for "battery" or
  "autopilot". After Compare, each control shows the relation of your profile that uses it. Covers DCS World,
  Falcon BMS, IL-2, Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020/2024, X-Plane 12, War Thunder, Nuclear Option and Star Citizen.
- **Follow input on the Relations page:** press a button or move an axis and the Relations page jumps to its
  relation, like the Binds page.
- The "N aircraft" chip of a relation lists all its aircraft when you hover it.

## Fixed
- Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024: export failed with "The specified file could not be encrypted" on some PCs.
  Exports and backups of all games now copy files in a way that works on every drive.
- After an import, the Start page kept its game cards hidden until JoyPro was restarted.
- Relation cards cut off the modifiers of a bind; device, input and every modifier are shown now.
- After jumping to a relation by pressing a button, a tooltip popped up at once; it now waits for the mouse.
- The aircraft count of a relation counted some aircraft twice.

## Changed
- Fonts fall back better for symbols and for Chinese, Japanese and Korean on printed cheat sheets and cockpit pages.
