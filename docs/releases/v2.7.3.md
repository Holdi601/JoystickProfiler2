# JoyPro 2.7.3

## New
- **Fate of the Galaxy (experimental).** The Star Wars total conversion for FreeSpace Open, installed with
  Knossos.NET, is a new game: JoyPro finds portable Knossos folders on any drive and the installed launcher's library,
  builds the list of controls from the installed mod (its own texts, without the controls the mod switches off),
  imports the controls your pilot uses and exports yours as the controls preset **JoyPro_FotG**, which it selects for
  your pilot and its campaign saves (backups first). Up to four devices (the game's Device 1-4), 128 buttons and the
  first hat per device; a device without a slot takes a free one. Dead zone and sensitivity are game settings (one for
  all axes), axis invert per bind. The watcher, Check, support file, axis curves, Auto Group, Auto Setup and the
  search know the game; space sim essentials (pitch, roll, yaw, throttle, lasers, missiles, weapon banks, targeting,
  match speed, energy, shields, views) are placed on your stick and throttle, never behind a JoyPro shift button (the
  game has none for joysticks), and grouped with the matching controls of Star Citizen, Elite Dangerous and X4
  (forward / reverse thrust with X4's, S-foils with Elite's hardpoints). Without a pilot yet, the export writes the
  preset and says to pick it in the game's controls menu.

## Fixed
- **Auto setup keeps the fire buttons.** When any free button or hat direction is left, primary fire, secondary fire
  and (in space games) target nearest hostile always get one, before views or comms. A printed grip label (for example
  electric trim) only keeps its button when your games have that function.
- **No binds behind a JoyPro shift button in games without joystick modifiers** (X-Plane, Arma, Fate of the Galaxy):
  snap views and flares used to land there and were then left out of the export.
- **Watcher help texts:** the hover help of **Keep my JoyPro binds** and **Take over anyway** now explains the new
  warning when taking over would remove many binds.
