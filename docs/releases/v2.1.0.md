# JoyPro 2.1.0

Telemetry recording for ten games, and cockpit pictures for many more aircraft, downloaded when you need them.

## New
- **Telemetry recording** (Settings > Telemetry recording, off until you switch it on): every flight or round is
  recorded with all the telemetry the game sends, in the background and compressed. Export a session for Tacview
  (ACMI) or as CSV, with a small timeline preview. Works with DCS World, IL-2 Great Battles and Korea, War Thunder,
  X-Plane 11/12, Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020/2024, Falcon BMS and Elite Dangerous; Tacview export where the game
  sends a position. Recordings stay on your PC.
- **Cockpit pictures for many more aircraft:** besides DCS World and X-Plane 12, the Cockpits page now has pictures
  for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 and 2024, IL-2 Great Battles, Nuclear Option and War Thunder: overview,
  stick, throttle and panels with numbered callouts next to the HOTAS tables.
- **Picture packs:** cockpit pictures are downloaded per aircraft when you open it (Settings > Privacy: ask, always
  or never), or for all your aircraft at once. Building them from your own games still works too.
- New texts in all 38 languages.

## Changed
- DCS World: the telemetry hook is now version 2 (position, world objects and events for recording). JoyPro shows
  the old hook as outdated: press **Set up** on the Force Feedback page once.
- X-Plane 11/12 telemetry reads datarefs directly (X-Plane 12: allow incoming connections in its network settings).

## Notes
- Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 telemetry needs the `SimConnect.dll` from the MSFS SDK.
