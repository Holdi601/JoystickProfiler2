# JoyPro 2.6.2

## New
- **Add actions: Same function and Related.** Below the actions whose name has every word you typed, the list now has
  two parts instead of one "Same meaning": **Same function** (the action does what your words mean: "thrust" finds the
  throttles, "make the plane go faster" the throttle up) and **Related** (looser links: a word of the action's
  category, some of your words, a similar word). Related is folded with its count; a click on it shows the actions.
- **Add actions: why was it found?** Hover the label on a row: it says it in plain words, for example "'brakes' is
  understood as 'Wheel brakes'; this action's function is 'Wheel brakes axis'", "'thrust' is a synonym of 'Throttle'"
  or "Related: its category 'Gears, Brakes, Hook' has the word 'brakes'". The labels "word match" and
  "Title match (32%)" are gone.
- **Add actions: Same meaning on or off.** The new switch next to All / Axes / Buttons: off, only the actions whose name
  has every word are listed, and the line above the list says how many are hidden. When no name has every word, the
  list says so and **Show same meaning** switches it back on in one click. The switch is remembered and is also in
  Settings > Relations.
- **Add actions starts empty.** Opening a relation no longer puts its name into the search box. Type what you want to
  add, or use **Suggested for this relation**, which still suggests by the relation itself.
- **Axis response: exact numbers.** The 11 points of the user curve are number boxes now (0 % ... 100 % of the stick
  travel) instead of a second row of sliders. Type the value, or press Up / Down (Shift: ten steps) or turn the mouse
  wheel in the box; the white points on the curve and the boxes always show the same. Dead zone, saturation X/Y and
  curvature show their range next to the box ("0 .. 1"), and a number outside it goes back to the nearest end.
- **Copy to another axis.** **Copy to another axis...** in the axis response puts the same dead zone, saturation,
  curve and slider on another bound axis in one step, for example Thrust Left to Thrust Right (the other side is
  offered first). Invert goes along only when you tick **Copy invert too**, because left and right often differ
  there. One Ctrl+Z takes it back.
- **Relations: the aircraft filter in the open relation.** With the Games filter on an aircraft ("DCS World:
  F-100D"), the open relation's actions for that aircraft come first with a blue edge and **Filter: F-100D** on their
  aircraft line; the other actions are folded in **Other aircraft (n)** below, a click opens them. The relation stays
  complete, so nothing is changed by mistake; clearing the filter shows the plain list again.

## Fixed
- **Refresh DB no longer switches on aircraft you never bound.** After the command lists changed (Refresh DB, or an
  update with new lists), JoyPro used to switch on every other aircraft that shares a command with one of your actions:
  a helicopter power lever relation with your AH-64D, Ka-50 III and Mosquito suddenly also had the F-15C, A-10C,
  MiG-29, Su-25 and more, and an export would have given all of them a second throttle axis. Now JoyPro only adds an
  aircraft that is new in the lists (a module you installed since), never one that was there and that you did not
  choose, and never "Default". An aircraft that only gets a new command through a DCS patch or a mod is left as you set
  it up.
- **Your profile is put back if this already happened.** When you open a profile, JoyPro looks in its history for that
  step. If it finds one, a notice under the bar says "JoyPro added 448 aircraft to 180 relations on 03.11.2026 after
  the command lists changed", with the list per relation and aircraft. **Take them out** takes the ticked ones out again
  (one Ctrl+Z brings them back), **Keep these aircraft** leaves them and stops asking, **Later** asks the next time.
  Nothing changes until you choose.
- **What JoyPro changes by itself is its own step in the History.** When JoyPro adjusts the profile to new command
  lists, that is saved as "Updated to the new command lists: 3 aircraft in 2 relations" before your next change, so
  Ctrl+Z takes exactly it back. Before, it was saved under the name of your next click. Previews (suggestions, dry runs)
  no longer save anything.
- **The question "does different things in different aircraft" is clear now.** It names the button, the aircraft and
  what the button does in each ("BTN 20 does different things in these aircraft: F/A-18C: RADAR Switch - EMERG ·
  F-16C: KY-58 MODE Knob - RV. Where should BTN 20 work?"), and the buttons say what happens: **Only in the F/A-18C
  (take it off the F-16C)**, **Keep it in both (F-16C keeps KY-58 MODE Knob - RV)**, **Decide later**. "Keep it in
  both" changes nothing in the profile, it only stops asking on this PC (its tooltip says so).
- **The question no longer proposes to take out your own binds.** An aircraft that came from your game files (an
  import) is never the one it suggests to drop, and a helicopter's engine throttle counts as the same as the throttle
  or power levers of the other aircraft: the Ka-50 III throttle is no longer flagged next to the Apache's power levers.
- **An action with different meanings per aircraft shows its main meaning as the title**, the other meanings on lines
  of their own ("F-16C: KY-58 MODE Knob - RV (different function)", "also called: Thrust (13), Power Levers (Both)
  (2)") instead of one long comma list.
- **Add actions: "brakes" and the landing gear switches.** In a relation like "Wheel Brakes (analog)", searching
  "brakes" in DCS showed the F-4E's landing gear, arresting hook and anti-skid switches as "Same meaning". They were
  found only because their category is "Gears, Brakes, Hook"; they are now under Related (folded) and say so. The
  wheel brake axes were always found: they are at the top under "Name has every word", and the word "Brake" in their
  title is now marked for "brakes" too.
- **Search: singular and plural.** "switches" now finds "Switch" the way "brakes" finds "Brake" (also "flashes",
  "passes", "boxes").
- **Add actions: the F-4E's "[SimPit] ... (Switch)" actions** were labelled just "axis". DCS really lists them as axes
  (for home cockpits that wire a switch as an axis); they now say "axis (sim-pit switch)" with an explanation.
- **Add actions:** the line under a row's title showed a stray quote ("same function: Throttle axis' ('Throttle')").
- **Binds: the axis response opens by itself** when you pick an axis. If you close it, it stays closed for the next
  axis (also after a restart) until you open it again.
- **Binds: Modifiers on this bind has a close button.** The x closes the panel down to its title line; **Show** opens
  it again, and JoyPro remembers your choice.
