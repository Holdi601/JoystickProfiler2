# JoyPro 2.7.4

## Fixed
- **Fate of the Galaxy no longer stops at start after an export** ("JSON element signature must be an integer"). JoyPro
  kept its own notes next to your pilot as a `.json` file, and the game tries to load every `.json` in its players folder
  as a pilot. The notes now have a name the game ignores; the next export moves the old file and removes it. To start the
  game right away, delete `<pilot>.json.joypro-meta.json` in `data\players` of your FotG user folder.
- **Fate of the Galaxy throttle direction.** The absolute throttle came out reversed (lever forward = less thrust).
  JoyPro now writes it as the game expects it - forward is more thrust, as in DCS - and still writes the game's invert
  flag when you invert the axis yourself. Export once more; a re-import of the old export keeps your direction.
- **Detent mode: the throttle never falls any more.** A bent DCS curve could dip somewhere (the MiG-21 curve gave less
  at 80 % than at 70 %), also when the relation's own curve dips. Every curve detent mode makes now rises along the
  whole travel, for every aircraft.
- **Detent mode: both engines stay out of the afterburner while the levers rest in their detents.** The afterburner
  now starts clearly past the detent: resting in the detent, or up to 2 % of the travel past it (the yellow strip in
  the curve), stays below it, and two levers give the same throttle at the same place relative to their own detent.
  Resting in the detent gives the afterburner start minus 2 % (was 1 %).
