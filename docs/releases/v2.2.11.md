# JoyPro 2.2.11

Per-aircraft binds for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024, and add-on aircraft.

## New
- **Per-aircraft binds in MSFS 2024, like in DCS:** relations of MSFS 2024 get aircraft checkmarks. The export writes
  every profile of your own the sim uses, each with the relations ticked for all of its aircraft; a relation ticked
  for only some aircraft of a shared profile is reported by name, with how to give that aircraft its own profile.
- **Import stays general:** imported relations apply to all aircraft. Binds the sim has only in the profiles of some
  aircraft are listed after the import, with "Limit to these aircraft" per relation, "Limit all" and "Keep for all
  aircraft".
- **Add-on aircraft:** the DB refresh reads the input presets of unpacked add-on aircraft (Community folder,
  Marketplace) and adds their own actions, marked with their aircraft. Actions only some aircraft's profiles have
  come in the same way on import. In the relation editor such an action shows "only for: <aircraft>", and adding it
  ticks just those aircraft.

## Changed
- The export result shows each MSFS 2024 profile with its aircraft and the number of binds written.
