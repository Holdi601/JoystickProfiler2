# JoyPro 2.3.0

## Much faster
- JoyPro keeps its engine running in the background instead of starting it for every click. Opening a relation,
  searching, suggestions, adding actions and loading a profile now take well under two seconds (a relation with one
  action: from about 8 s to about 1 s; search: from 1.5 s to under 0.1 s; profile load: from 7 s to 1.5 s).
- DCS export: from about 73 s to about 3–4 s (clean + nuke: from about 2.5 min to under 10 s). DB refreshes run in
  parallel: DCS from several minutes to well under a minute, MSFS 2024 from about a minute to about 2 s.
- Adding hundreds of actions at once works and stays fast.

## New
- **Find by meaning**: search, the catalog filter, the JoyPro assistant, Edit with AI and By description understand
  synonyms, slang and plain sentences ("fast travel", "drop flares", "the button that makes my ship go really far
  really fast"), also in German, for every function and every game, without an AI model.
- **The assistant and Edit with AI work for every game**, not only DCS: "make a relation for quantum jump in Star
  Citizen", "add the flaps of xp12", "what is boost in Elite Dangerous", "put the gear of every game into Gear".
- **Pages**: hide pages of the bottom bar you don't use and change their order (Settings > Pages). A hidden page still
  opens from links and can be shown again from there.
- **Reorder actions in a relation**: drag them by the handle, Alt+Up/Down, or the right-click menu.
- **Duplicate without bind**, and a right-click menu on relation cards (Duplicate, Duplicate without bind, Delete).
- **Sort levels in the catalog and the search**: game, aircraft, category, title, axis/button, last updated.
- **Layout**: drag an unplaced input straight onto the picture; "Now place: ..." walks through the rest; every placement
  can be undone.
- **2D layouts**: the text positions of the JoyPro 1 sheets and of community sheets by maogowzeg (Discord) for WinWing
  Orion 2 devices. JoyPro offers a sheet when its picture is on your PC: your JoyPro 1 folder, or the community sheets
  folder (Layout > Use your own picture > Open the community sheets folder).
- **Share layout** includes a page with several views.
- Catalogs for ACE COMBAT 7/8 and Battlefield 6/2042 until JoyPro reads the game's own list.

## Fixed
- MSFS 2024 (and other games): the catalog showed "0 actions" and searches found nothing when a game text contained
  typographic quotes; suggestions could disappear ("JoyPro does not know this function yet") for the same reason.
- Readable names everywhere: no raw ids in the catalog, search, suggestions, binds, layout tables and exports. DCS
  modules with localization keys (Spitfire, Bf 109, FW 190, Mosquito, P-47, Yak-52, CH-47F, MiG-29 Fulcrum, ...),
  Star Citizen, MSFS 2020, IL-2, X-Plane, Elite Dangerous and others now show the game's own names and categories.
  Searching by a raw key or id still finds the action.
- Removing an old device no longer deletes binds and modifiers of other devices with the same name; deleting,
  merging or replacing relations or devices keeps modifiers that are still in use.
- Light mode: buttons and texts that were hard to read (dark text on dark blue and others).
- The local AI model stops after 30 idle minutes by default, and never while it is still working.
- Relation editor: cards no longer overlap when a card grows; buttons no longer slide under the search box on
  narrow windows; a new relation no longer searches for its placeholder name.
- Catalog: categories most relevant to the open relation come first; the "another action for the same thing" hint
  appears only for real twins, never for opposites like increase/decrease.
- X4 steering axes have readable names (yaw, roll, pitch); full game names instead of short ones.
- Star Citizen export writes to the chosen folder.
- The Binds page's quick view lists the relation's actions again.
