# JoyPro 2.4.2

## Fixed
- A bind you add in DCS on a button that other aircraft already use now joins that button's relation instead of
  becoming a separate relation. This works when JoyPro takes over the game's changes and when you import from DCS with
  "Add to my profile". Only when no relation uses the button yet, or that aircraft already has another action on it,
  does the bind get a relation of its own. After taking over, JoyPro says which relation each change went into.
- The search on the Relations and Binds pages ("Name + actions") now finds relations by the titles of their actions,
  for example "Master" finds the relation with "Toggle Master Warning". Before, it only found relation names for
  actions that came in through an import or a take-over, in DCS and in MSFS alike.

## New
- Move one action to another button: right-click an action in the relation editor and choose "Move to another
  button...", or right-click a bind row on the Binds page. Press the new button (or pick the device and input) and
  press Move. The action goes into the relation that already uses that button, or gets a relation of its own; the
  other actions stay where they are. One Undo takes it back.
