# JoyPro 2.6.5

## New
- **Tick the aircraft a curve is for.** The axis response no longer asks you to pick a "scope" from a list. **Applies
  to** shows all games, each game of the relation and each DCS aircraft with a checkbox, and next to each what it uses
  today: "own curve", "same as AH-64D BLK.II Pilot", "uses DCS". When you open a relation, the aircraft that use DCS's
  curve are ticked (on a new relation: all of them); aircraft with their own curve stay unticked, so you do not overwrite
  them by accident.
- **Save says what it will do.** "Save stores it for DCS, all its aircraft (5)" keeps one curve for DCS, so these
  aircraft follow it later too. Untick two and it says "Save stores it for these aircraft (3 of 5): ...": those three get
  the curve, the other two keep theirs. If you tick an aircraft that has its own curve, an orange line says that its curve
  will be replaced, before you save. Each save is one step: one Ctrl+Z takes it back. **Use the game's curve again** does
  the same for the ticked aircraft.
- **JoyPro remembers what you did.** Under the list, **Curves now** groups the aircraft by the curve they share
  ("AH-64D CPG, AH-64D Pilot, Ka-50 III: same curve", "SA342 Copilot, SA342 Pilot: uses DCS"); a click ticks a group.
  The aircraft you last saved for come back ticked when you open the relation again, also after a restart. **Copy to
  another axis** copies to the same aircraft.
- **From a relation to its bind.** The bind under a relation's name ("MFD CENTER · BTN 11", now with a small arrow) is
  a link: a click opens the Binds page with that bind selected, and **Back** (Alt+Left) takes you back to the relation.
  The other way round was already there: **Open in Relations** on the Binds page.

## Fixed
- **The aircraft you filter for come first.** With the Games filter on "DCS World: F-16C_50", an action for the F-100D and
  the F-16C_50 still named the F-100D first everywhere: "Thrust · DCS World · F-100D +1 more" under the relation's name,
  "F-100D, F-16C_50" in its row, the F-100D's icon, and the F-100D on top of its aircraft list. Now the F-16C_50 leads:
  "F-16C bl.50 +1 more", "F-16C_50, F-100D", the F-16C_50's icon, and in the aircraft list it is first under
  "Filter: F-16C_50", then the others as always. The Binds page does the same with its own Games filter (the search lines
  and "What it does"). Nothing is hidden and your profile does not change: only the order you see. Without a filter
  everything looks as before.
- **Open catalog follows the Games filter too.** With "DCS World: F-16C_50" filtered, **Open catalog** on a relation for
  the F-100D and the F-16C_50 opened the F-100D's list. Now it opens the F-16C_50's (or the filtered game's) when the
  relation has it. Without a filter it opens the relation's first aircraft as before.
- **History: the button now says what it does.** "Restore" on a version is now **Roll back to here**: the profile
  becomes exactly as it was at that point, and every change after it is taken back in one step. Clicking the version
  five changes ago takes back all five. The later versions stay in the list, and one **Undo** (Ctrl+Z) brings them all
  back. The question before it and the line at the bottom use the same words.

## New (experimental)
- **One relation, several inputs.** Settings > Experimental > **Several inputs per relation** (off by default). Then a
  relation can be on more than one button or axis at once, each with its own device, modifiers and axis curve: the
  same grip on two bases, a second fire button, a throttle on two throttles. On the Binds page the relation shows
  **More inputs**: **Record another input**, **Make main**, **Remove**. **Copy every bind of a device to another device**
  puts everything of one device on the matching buttons of the other in one step (Preview first; Ctrl+Z takes it back).
  JoyPro matches the buttons like **Replace device** does; if your second base numbers the grip's buttons differently,
  press them once and change the few that differ.
- Exports write every input where the game can hold several per action (DCS, IL-2, FSX/P3D, Arma, X4 and others; MSFS,
  Elite, War Thunder and a few more up to their own limits). Games that keep one input per action (Star Citizen,
  Battlefield, G-Rebels, HLL Vietnam, VTOL VR native, WARDOGS, Ace Combat 8) get the main input, and the export names
  the relations. Importing your own export brings every input back into the same relation.
- With the switch off nothing changes: your profile, your exports and the pages stay exactly as before.
