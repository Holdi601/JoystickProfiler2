# JoyPro 2.4.3

## Fixed
- Move to another button: when the aircraft already has another action on the new button, the window no longer says
  "No relation uses that button yet". It now names the relation on that button and the action there, and lets you
  choose: swap the two actions, replace the one there, or keep both on the button (both then fire together).
- Pressing the new button in the Move window no longer makes the relation editor jump to the relation on that button,
  so the window and the editor show the same relation.
- A button pressed in the Move window is matched to your device as the profile names it, so the action always lands on
  the same device as your other relations.

- An import from a game right after a change in JoyPro (for example a moved action) no longer drops that change
  without a word. JoyPro says what would be lost ("You changed 2 binds for DCS that are not in DCS yet") and offers
  "Import (drop my changes for DCS)", "Export first" or "Cancel"; Undo or History brings dropped changes back.
  "Do not ask again" switches the question off (Settings > Confirmations switches it on again). With nothing
  unexported there is no question. When a game changes its controls, JoyPro offers only what the game changed, never
  to undo your own changes.
- The search's match lines name the aircraft as JoyPro does elsewhere ("AH-64D BLK.II", "F-16C bl.50") instead of
  their ids.

## New
- The search on the Relations and Binds pages shows what it found: under each relation the matching actions, with the
  word marked and their game and aircraft (for example "Master Caution Button · DCS World · AH-64D BLK.II (2)"), up to
  three lines and then "+N more". Relations whose action has your whole search come first. Click a line to open the
  relation at that action.
- Reporting a problem is one step now (Settings > Report a problem > Create Report): the zip holds your text and
  everything needed to find the problem, so you are not asked for more files afterwards: JoyPro's logs of at least the
  last 14 days, the last errors and crash reports, JoyPro's settings and versions, your devices with vJoy and HidHide,
  and per game its folders and JoyPro's backups. With the box "Include my open profile and its history" (on by
  default, remembered) it also holds the open profile, its saved versions and the games' current bind files. JoyPro
  shows what goes in before it saves the zip; your user name and user folder are replaced, also inside the profile
  copy, and the zip stays on your PC until you send it.
- JoyPro keeps its logs 30 days instead of 7 (at most 200 MB together, the oldest go first), so a report can show what
  happened further back. A number of days you set yourself stays.
