# JoyPro 2.3.1

## Please check your profile after updating
After an update to 2.2.17 or 2.3.0, some actions may have ended up in two relations at once. A DCS export then binds
them on two inputs. This version fixes the cause. To clean your profile: open the **Export** page, press **Check**,
then **Fix the sure ones** (or go through the cards one by one), and export again. Every fix can be undone in one
step.

## Fixed
- Actions were sometimes switched on in a second relation when JoyPro added newer aircraft to your relations after an
  update. JoyPro now never does that on its own, and an update of JoyPro's device pictures or catalogs no longer starts
  this step at all.
- Imports and the change watcher tell you when an action would end up in two relations.
- IL-2 Great Battles and IL-2 Korea show the game's own control names and explain what each control does.
- Abbreviations in MSFS 2024, Star Citizen and X-Plane action names are written out (for example Electronic Flight Bag).
- The first change after opening a profile is faster.

## New
- **Problems found by the check**, redesigned: readable cards per pair of relations (action, aircraft, both relations
  and inputs), how sure JoyPro is about the fix, one-click fixes (keep in one relation, keep both, open both), "Fix the
  sure ones" with a slider, "Fix all with a rule" and "Fix with AI". Every change is previewed first.
- **Lay out with AI** on the Binds page: say how you like your controls ("throttle and left-hand stick", "trim on the
  hat, softer curves for helis", also in German) and JoyPro lays out binds, curves, force feedback and vJoy; preview
  first, one undo step.
- **Auto Assign** reworked: time-critical functions go on the grips, MFDs and labelled panels get only what is printed
  on them, directional sets (TMS, DMS, CMS, trim) stay together on one hat, and an optional mode switch gives an
  air-to-air and an air-to-ground layer.
- **Statistics** (switch it on in Settings): hours per game, aircraft and ship, top lists, streaks, landings, kills,
  jumps and more, read from your games' logs, journals, logbooks and Steam playtime. Everything stays on your PC.
- **Lay out new aircraft like my most used one**: Auto Assign, Auto Group and Lay out with AI can use your most flown
  aircraft as the example for modules you haven't set up yet.
- Loading profiles that were saved with older command lists is much faster.
