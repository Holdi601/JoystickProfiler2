# JoyPro 2.6.3

## Fixed
- **Copy to another axis: the Copy button did nothing.** It closed its window before the copy ran, so no axis got the
  settings. It works now; the settings of the axis you were on were never lost, so you can copy them again.
- **FSX and Prepar3D: elevator and elevator trim up and down had their titles swapped.** "Up" means nose up, as in
  Microsoft Flight Simulator. If you put these actions in a relation by their old title, check its direction.
- **Add actions: the action you searched for gets "Recommended", not its neighbour.** Searching "LMFD OSB 15" in Falcon
  BMS marked "FMFD: OSB-15 Button - Push" as Recommended, and "LMFD: OSB-15 Button - Push" said "Differs: another action
  for the same thing". Now the LMFD row is under **Name has every word**, with its words marked, and is the one
  recommended. "OSB15" and "OSB-15" find it the same way. Recommended in a search now goes by how well a row fits what
  you typed: a row that fits worse never gets it from one that fits better, and a row under Related never gets it.
- **A number or side you type counts.** When you name "15", "left", "right" or "LMFD", the actions without it are no
  longer "Same function": the other displays' OSB 15, the MFD switches or "APU Fire Pushbutton" are under **Related**
  (folded), and a row says what it lacks ("its title does not have 'LMFD'"). "LMFD" also means "Left MFD", so DCS's
  "Left MFD OSB 15" comes first in the F-16. In the DCS F-16, "OSB 15" no longer finds the radar warning receiver because
  of its "SPO-15". "thrust" still finds the throttles, "gear up" still finds a "Toggle Gear", and "yaw left" still finds
  the rudder axis.
- **"OSB 15" with no display named:** in Falcon BMS the OSB 15 of all four displays are recommended, none is called a
  variant of another. In the suggestions for a relation named "MFD LEFT BTN12", the LMFD's OSB is the recommended one.
- **Add actions: the game filter stays.** Choosing a game chip ("Falcon BMS") now lasts: when you open another relation,
  when you search again, and after a restart. If that game has nothing for a search, the list says so, tells you how many
  results the other games have, and **Show all games** switches the filter off in one click. The chips of **Suggested
  for this relation** stay the same way.
- **Relations: the Games filter on the left stays on.** Opening a relation the filter hides (from a tag, the check, a new
  relation) used to switch all filters off. Now they stay as you set them, and that one relation is listed until you
  change the filters.
- **"Already in this relation" for some of the aircraft.** An action a relation has for most of its aircraft looked like
  a new one: "F1 Cockpit view" in the relation "F1 Cockpit view" was an ordinary row with 20 of 20 aircraft ticked,
  because one aircraft was still missing. Now the row says "already in this relation for 19 of 20 aircraft" and offers
  only the missing aircraft; in its aircraft list the others are marked. With all aircraft in, it is greyed at the bottom
  as before.
- **"Which grip?" on rudder pedals.** Start page > Your devices asked "Which grip?" on devices that have no grip, and the
  Layout page gave no way to answer except the Model... menu. The chip now says **Which model?** when several products
  share a USB id, and a click on it opens the Layout page with that device and the choice open. Your answer stays after
  a restart and the chip goes away.
- **The notice about aircraft JoyPro added now says what it means, and comes up less often.** It said "not bound by you"
  and "a second bind", and counted "Default" as an aircraft, so a profile with nothing but "F1 Cockpit view: Default" got
  a warning about 17 aircraft. Now it says that an older JoyPro switched these on, what **Take them out** does and that
  your own binds stay. "Default" and DCS's other input layers (except the UI layer, whose binds work in every aircraft)
  are no aircraft: they are not counted, they come last in the list, and if there is nothing else you get no notice. The
  list reads better too: "'F1 Cockpit view': Default (not an aircraft)".
- **Your own imports are no longer offered for removal.** If the command lists had changed before an import (Refresh DB,
  an update), that notice could list aircraft you had really imported as "not bound by you". The same went for taking
  over changes made in a game. Imports and take-overs are never offered now.
- **MFG Crosswind V2/3 is no longer asked about.** The pedals call themselves both models, and both have the same inputs,
  so the answer would change nothing about your binds. If you want the other model's picture, Model... > Choose another
  model still does that.
