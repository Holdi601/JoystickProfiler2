Manage with AI / Edit with AI

From docs/ai-plans.md in the repository (generated, always the current text).

Optional and local only. A request in your own words becomes a plan of JoyPro's own relation steps. The local AI model (see natural-language-grouping.md) only proposes the plan; JoyPro checks every step against your profile and the catalog, shows it with a checkbox per step and runs the ticked steps when you press Apply, as one change: one Undo takes all of them back. Discard forgets the plan. Without the model JoyPro works as before, and a few simple requests still work through offline rules.

#Where

Every AI action shows the same status line of the local AI (its whole setup is Settings > Local AI): whether it is switched on and whether the model is loaded right now, e.g. "AI: on (Qwen3 8B) - not loaded right now, starts with your next request", with Turn on the local AI / Set up the local AI when it is off and a Settings link. Make a plan reads the setting first (2.2.15 sent the first request after a start with the AI off) and shows "Starting the AI model ..." while the model loads.

  • Manage with AI: the wand button in the Relations list header (between the title and New). The whole profile: "append _sc to every relation that contains Star Citizen", "delete all relations that have no bind", "new relation with pitch of all games but no helicopters".
  • Edit with AI: next to "Auto-build relations from all my actions..." for the open relation only: its actions, aircraft ticks, name, groups and axis tuning. Every step shows before and after; a step that would touch another relation is rejected.

#Steps

Step Fields What JoyPro checks
create name, kind (axis/button), describe the name is free; the actions come from describe through the Group by description rules
delete relations (exact names) each name exists
rename relation, to the relation exists, the new name is free
add / remove relation, describe (or games for remove) the actions exist in the catalog and are of the relation's kind
aircraft relation, planes, on the aircraft are in the relation
groups relation, add, remove something changes
tuning relation, curvature, deadzone, saturation, invert an axis relation with a bind; values clamped
fill planes (names, or "all DCS aircraft"), relations a preview, see below
check relations (none: all) every action against all its ticked aircraft (relations check-aircraft): where the id does something else there, that aircraft is unticked; nothing found: a preview line

Offline rules (no model): append / prepend a part to every relation that contains a game (its actions) or a word (its name), delete relations without a bind, rename A to B ("rename it to ..." in Edit with AI), and fill / check: a request about the relations with check words (check, verify, go through, review, make sure, fulfil, works properly) becomes a check step; one with fill words (fill, add, expand, extend, update, include, bring) that names aircraft ("... the new modules: Mirage F1EE, F-15E Strike Eagle, C-130J": after a colon, in a list, with "and") becomes a fill step of those aircraft; "all modules / aircraft in DCS" without names fills every DCS aircraft.

The model and the rules (--llm auto, what the app sends while the local AI is on): the exact rules (rename, prefix/append, delete without binds) run without the model. Everything else goes to the model, with JoyPro's own reading of fill/check as a hint; the plan keeps the check and fill steps the rules read even when the model leaves them out. When the model does not start or answer, or answers with no steps for a request that names aircraft, JoyPro makes the plan from its own reading and says why (with the model's reply). A model that does not start says why in plain words (not enough memory, a llama.cpp too old for the model, a damaged GGUF file, ...; a model kept loaded for the app is started once more with its output read to get that reason). --llm off (the local AI switched off) uses the rules only; without a rule the answer says to switch the AI on in Settings > Local AI.

#Fill aircraft into the relations (mass edit)

"Check the modules released since 2023, i.e. F-15E Strike Eagle, F-4E Phantom II, OH-58D, CH-47F, F4U-1D, MiG-29A, C-130J, La-7, F-100D, and fill them into the relations where they fit" becomes one fill step (offline rule or the model; the model only names the aircraft). The aircraft names are resolved against the catalog (every seat: F-15E, F-15E_WSO); names JoyPro does not know (a pack like "Flaming Cliffs 2024", a module not in the catalog yet) are listed. Other games work the same way by their name ("Star Citizen").

Per relation x aircraft, two independent paths:

  1. Rules (always): Auto Group's slot matching over the manual-checked function catalog, restricted to the named aircraft and run on the profile in memory (taken back at once), then Suggest missing for relations that span many aircraft but got nothing (20 s cap). 400+ relations take about half a minute.
  2. AI (local model on; --llm on checks every pair, auto only the uncertain ones): JoyPro shows the model the relation (name, kind, its actions) and the aircraft's candidates (the rule picks plus a catalog search filtered to that aircraft), each as a small knowledge record: readable title, cockpit device/category, axis or button, function slot, and what it does from JoyPro's own data (cockpit guide text, the sim's description, the function's general explanation) or "no manual data". The model picks one candidate or none with a reason; only candidate ids count. Batches of 5 pairs, 120 s cap, picks cached per relation x aircraft in <data>\Llm\fill-cache.json.

Combined: both agree = high (ticked); one path = medium (rules: ticked, AI only: unticked until you agree); different picks = ambiguous (both shown, unticked). The preview shows a summary ("57 relations get 312 actions for 10 aircraft; 4 relations ambiguous; 23 functions found no match"), the relations (expandable) with each action, its aircraft, the path (Rules / AI / Rules + AI) and the reason, a checkbox per relation and per action, and filters by aircraft and confidence. Apply selected hands the ticked actions back as add steps with exact specs (one history step).

Knowledge records per game (explanations, relations action-info --coverage, without the local cockpit pack): MSFS 2020/2024 rich (the sim's own descriptions, 90-99%); FSX/P3D, Arma 3, Arma Reforger 84-93% (general function texts); DCS 52%, X-Plane 61-64%, IL-2 54-69%, War Thunder 61%, VTOL VR 64%, Falcon BMS 56%; thin: Star Citizen 32%, Elite 41%, Project Wingman 40%, Nuclear Option 44%, X4 45%. With the cockpit pack installed the cockpit guide adds the per-control texts (mainly DCS).

#Every game

Every AI path (the JoyPro assistant, Manage / Edit with AI, Expand with AI = fill, By description, the relation chat) understands every game JoyPro has a command list for, not only DCS:

  • Game names in a request, full or short ("X-Plane 12", "xp12", "msfs" = 2024 and 2020, "wt", "sc", "elite", "x4", "bms", "IL-2 1946", "Cliffs of Dover", "ACE COMBAT 7", "p3d"): EngineGameWords reads them from DB/Functions/language.json and the game integrations, so the actions come from that game only. "every game" means all.
  • Offline rules (no model): "make a relation for landing lights in X-Plane 12" (create, named "Landing Lights (X-Plane 12)"), "put the landing gear of every game into Gear" (add, or create when Gear does not exist), "expand Roll with Star Citizen" (fill of that game into Roll), in Edit with AI "add MSFS" / "also War Thunder" (the open relation's function there) and "add the flaps of X-Plane 12", "remove War Thunder from Throttle".
  • Questions ("what does Landing gear down do in X-Plane 12", "what is boost in Elite") get JoyPro's answer and change nothing: the action's readable title, the game, axis or button, its category, what it does (cockpit guide, the game's own description or the function's general text; "the game has no description of it" otherwise) and the relations that have it. Without and with the model (the model would only repeat it).
  • Ships: Star Citizen, Elite Dangerous and X4 have their words (boost, strafe up/down/left/right, landing gear, jump drive: FSD, supercruise, quantum, travel drive); a ship's boost is the afterburner function there.
  • What the model sees: every prompt names the games with a command list (name = id), the games the request names and, per named game, its actions for the request's words by readable title; the assistant's resolve, search and suggest take games, and a call without them keeps to the games the request names. The fill prompt says the target is a DCS aircraft or a whole game. By description shows example commands of the named games first.
  • No ids in any answer: actions by readable title (Star Citizen "v_toggle_landing_system" = "Toggle landing system"), games by name ("X-Plane 12", not "XPlane12"), categories in words.

engine --profile p.pr0file relations ai-matrix [--games g1|g2] [--paths p1|p2] [--examples N] asks every path typical requests for every game (flaps, landing gear, pitch, landing lights; boost, strafe up, jump drive for the space games; short names; questions; what the model would be shown) on the profile in memory, judges each answer (the named game's actions only, a readable answer, an answer at all; a game without the function gets none of another game's) and saves nothing. JOYPRO_AI_MATRIX_CONTEXT=1 adds the whole model context to the answers.

#Command line

engine --profile p.pr0file relations plan ask "<request>" [--relation <name>] [--llm auto|on|off]
engine --profile p.pr0file relations plan apply (--file plan.json | '<json>') [--relation <name>] [--only 0,2] [--dry-run]

ask returns the plan (Ops) and the checked steps (Steps: Text, Ok, Error, Before, After, Details) and changes nothing. apply runs the steps of {"ops":[...]} (only those of --only), saves the profile once (one history step) unless --dry-run. --relation keeps every step inside that relation.
