Release notes

JoyPro 2.6.0

From docs/releases/v2.6.0.md in the repository (generated, always the current text).

#New

  • Relations page: the filters (groups, games, devices, bound / not bound, axes / buttons, sort and what the search looks at) fold up under "Filters", so the list gets the room on a 1280x800 screen. Folded, the line says which filters are on and how many relations are shown. Open or folded is kept after a restart.

  • Layout page, experimental and off by default: the marker assistant can ring the spots of a photo that look like buttons, hats or switches (More > Detected spots (experimental)). A marker you place near a ring goes onto its centre. JoyPro does not know which input a spot is and places nothing by itself; it finds about six of ten controls, axes and dark controls on a dark device least. Works offline, nothing is downloaded.

  • Virtual joystick: modes like in Joystick Gremlin. Give a game's virtual functions named modes (Combat, Landing ...) that inherit from each other, and switch them with a button: switch, hold, toggle, cycle or back. An input can do something else in every mode.

  • Virtual joystick: a function can read another function's output (an axis band that latches a toggle, a curve that feeds a split). JoyPro refuses chains that would go round in a loop.

  • Virtual joystick: new functions "Remap" (an input straight to one or more virtual outputs, also keys) and "Mode switch".

  • Virtual joystick: the Functions tab shows all functions of a game as a graph. Drag an output onto an input to chain two functions, click a wire to remove it, remove or open a function from its node.

  • Coming from Joystick Gremlin: import your Gremlin profile (R13 or R14) on the Functions tab. Modes, remaps, response curves, keyboard mappings, macros, merged axes, tempo, double tap, chain, toggle and hat buttons become virtual functions. Before anything is saved you see what was imported, what works a little differently, and what JoyPro cannot do.

  • HUD, show and hide everything: every widget can be shown on your screen and in the stream separately, and every part of a widget can be switched off (name, kind, picture, buttons, held buttons by name, axes, axis names and values, stick position, hats, the header's picture). Everything is kept after a restart.

  • HUD styles: 40 presets (from Glass, Neon and Minimal to Military green HUD, Amber MFD, Airliner PFD, Synthwave, Retro terminal, High contrast and more) and design options for each part: cards, text and font, the shape of the stick element (ring, square, crosshair, radar, dot), of bars and levers (rounded, square, thin, segmented, outline), buttons and chips. Save your own styles, export them as a file to share, import them, and give a single widget a style of its own.

  • HUD, the aircraft's own stick and throttle: a new widget shows the stick and throttle of the aircraft you fly as the game draws them (from the cockpit pictures of your games or a picture pack, else JoyPro's drawing of the grips). They tilt and slide with your stick and throttle, and the control of the button you press lights up (a Warthog's TMS up lights the A-10C's TMS). Pick the aircraft yourself or let DCS tell JoyPro.

  • Streamer mode: a page for OBS's Browser Source (http://127.0.0.1:8765/hud, on this PC only) shows the HUD with a truly transparent background, live, with the same styles; no custom CSS needed. Or a stream window for Window Capture that may sit behind the game, transparent or on a chroma key colour. Your own screen and the stream are independent: play without a HUD while the stream shows it.

  • HUD performance: the HUD updates at most 30 times a second (15, 30 or 60 to choose), only when something changed; the stream page costs nothing while OBS is not connected.

  • Cockpits page: photos for cockpits no game has pictures of. For 40 aircraft, helicopters, spacecraft and ships (A350, A380, 777, ATR 72, E-Jets, CRJ, Q400, UH-60M, CH-47D, NH90, F-22, F-35, Apollo, Space Shuttle and more) Download photos fetches every photo of the cockpit in one click, with progress, checks each one and takes the web archive's copy when a site no longer has it. The manual then gets a page per photo with JoyPro's numbered labels for the main panels on top (a click on a label opens that panel's page), and the photo's author, licence and site below. The photos are kept on your PC only.

  • Cockpits page: the MH-60R / MH-60S Seahawk gets three photos with labels: its cockpit in flight and the MH-60R's centre console with the sonar and stabilator controls.

  • Cockpits page: ships and aircraft that exist only in a game (Star Citizen, Nuclear Option) show the cockpit JoyPro rendered from your game files where a photo would be, as the first picture under "Photos". It is made on your PC and stays there; nothing is downloaded and nothing is asked. The same goes for any cockpit without a photo whose game JoyPro reads. The Star Citizen Carrack, Nomad, Defender, C2 Hercules, Mercury Star Runner and Gladius, which have no real cockpit to photograph, now show their render there.

  • Check, "Same command on two buttons": Replace '...' on

  • Back to the check: a relation opened from a Check card shows Back to the check above its name; it returns to the card (the check runs again after a change).

  • Add actions and suggestions, used elsewhere: an action switched on in other relations has one tag: in 'Button CP2 B1' opens that relation, in 3 relations lists them and a name opens it; Back to '...' returns to your relation and search.

#Fixed

  • DCS export is faster: a file that would get exactly what it already has is no longer rewritten and copied into the backup first, so exporting again after a small change takes about half the time on a big Saved Games folder. "Undo last export" still undoes the export you just did, also one that changed nothing.
  • Add actions search: an action whose title has every word you typed is found and listed first. "tacan channel (xx0x) - inc" now finds "TACAN Channel (xx0x) - [Inc]" first (brackets and punctuation do not matter), "master" finds every title with "master" (35 in the F-14, also "MASTER RESET" and "Master Test Selector"), "master reset" lists "MASTER RESET" first, "reset" finds it too. Before, a short or common word only found the actions JoyPro understood by their meaning.
  • DCS actions ending in a tag such as [Inc], [Next], [Toggle] or [CW] were taken as "decrease" in the search, the suggestions and the "same function" notes. They now mean what the tag says. A "-" or "+" typed on its own at the end of a search ("tacan channel (xx0x) -") is no longer read as "decrease".
  • Add actions: actions already in the open relation are always shown, greyed at the bottom without a tick box, and the count says "+ 1 already in this relation". Before, some searches showed them at their place and others not at all.
  • Add actions is fast on big profiles: a search over every game with many results took a second and more per keystroke to show (on a 440-relation DCS profile: 0.6-1.7 s, now about 0.1 s); only the rows in view are built now.
  • Switched-off buttons and check boxes are readable in the Light and the Dark theme (the Start page's card of a game that is not found, for example); their icons fade with the text.
  • The force feedback page and the telemetry recording settings no longer list games you hid. A hidden game is not looked for there either; Settings > Games shows it again.
  • The Check cards' "More" and recommended buttons are as high as the buttons next to them.
  • Texts that were still English in the other languages are translated: merging copied relations, the DCS device options, and "Back" in about 25 languages. Serbian (Cyrillic) keeps key names such as Ctrl+Z in Latin letters.
  • DCS: the C-130J's overhead, pedestal and glareshield panels show their labelled faces in the cockpit pictures (they were plain grey); the throttle gets its own page. Comes with the next cockpit picture update.
  • Maker photos of devices: 12 more photos work again (VirtualFly Yoko Force Neo and Switcho Radios, PFC yokes and rudder pedals: the makers moved them to new addresses).
  • Maker photos of devices: 100 dead photo addresses work again (the same picture at the maker's new address or from the web archive).
  • Maker photos of devices: 770 photos (114 before) now have a web archive copy on record that is checked to be exactly the photo; when a maker's address stops working, JoyPro shows that copy instead. Comes with the next photo list update.
  • Cockpits page: every cockpit photo JoyPro knows (all 40) now has a checked web archive copy, so it still shows when its site removes it.
  • The virtual joystick forwarder now runs at below-normal priority, and rests while nothing moves: about 10 checks a second instead of the display's refresh rate.
  • The virtual joystick forwarder costs less CPU with sticks that report all the time while nobody touches them (force feedback bases, slightly noisy axes): it works at most once per screen refresh instead of up to twice plus on every report, reads only the devices that changed and writes the virtual joystick at most once per refresh. A turbo, pulse, long press or macro wakes it only when its next step is due. A button still goes out at once, or within one screen refresh while another device is streaming.
  • The vJoy device chosen in Settings as the one JoyPro tunnels into is now also the device the virtual joystick writes to: the mapping, the virtual functions, the forwarder and the Gremlin import start there, so a vJoy device below it that another program feeds (VoiceAttack, for example) is left alone. Imports of a game in virtual mode keep its binds on that device. The Virtual Joystick page shows the device and warns when the mapping needs a second vJoy device or no device is chosen.
  • A profile saved by a step that does not check the profile against the command lists (exchanging a device, for example) no longer counts as checked: the next time the profile is opened, the check against the current lists still runs.
  • Exchanging one vJoy device for another vJoy device works like any other device exchange.
  • Importing relations from a JSON file keeps the action names and categories the profile had stored (a profile no longer shrinks and shows ids where names were).
  • Auto Group: an aircraft switched off in one relation can still be placed in the relation of its function. Only a suggestion you dismissed for a relation, or an aircraft switched off there, keeps it out of that relation.
  • Auto Group: a valve, door, tank or seat "lock" is no longer taken for a target lock (the CH-47F's ramp valve lock no longer goes into "Radar Lock"); the same for "unlock", "reject" and "deselect".
  • Auto Group no longer makes one button do two things: when relations share a button, an aircraft that already uses the button in one of them is not added to another; the preview lists what was left out.
  • Auto Group follows your own substitutes (for example TDC depress on Radar Lock, or VoIP talk on a spare button) even where JoyPro's function catalog calls the command something else, and adds no second action next to yours.
  • Star Citizen: a device listed twice in the game's controls file, or once by the game's name and once by JoyPro's, no longer takes two of the eight joystick slots, so every connected device with binds gets a slot with the default export settings.
  • The Check no longer reports the plane's throttle and the helicopter's collective on one axis in War Thunder, MSFS and other games without separate aircraft as an error. Two actions of the same kind of vehicle on one button still are.
  • The vJoy device chosen in Settings as the one JoyPro tunnels into keeps its binds in every game now, also in Star Citizen, Elite Dangerous, IL-2, MSFS, War Thunder, X-Plane, Falcon BMS, Arma, WARDOGS, Nuclear Option and the other games, in every export mode ("Replace on my devices" and "Also clear my other devices" used to clear it outside DCS). The devices on your import's ignore list are left alone the same way.
  • Star Citizen: the export now also removes the old duplicate entries of a device from the game's controls file (the same stick listed under several joystick numbers), so they no longer use up the eight joystick slots. Binds on such an old entry move to the number the device keeps. Entries of devices that are not connected stay.
  • Star Citizen: "Replace on my devices" and "Also clear my other devices" left the ship binds of your connected devices in the game, and both "Update" modes kept the game's bind on a button you gave another action, so one button did two things. They now release them as described; devices that are not connected still keep theirs.
  • IL-2: a bind with a modifier on another device (a stick button with the throttle's pinky switch, for example) belongs to the device of its button: "Replace on my devices" replaces it and "Also clear my other devices" clears it like the device's other binds. It used to stay in every mode.
  • The Check also tells ground vehicles and ships apart from aircraft: one axis on the plane's throttle and on a tank's, car's or ship's throttle (War Thunder, Arma, Star Citizen's ground vehicles, IL-2 Cliffs of Dover's vehicles) is no error. Two actions of the same kind of vehicle on one axis still are.
  • Cockpits page: the notes, photos and sources below a grip page (the stick page, for example) no longer cover the page's table on a narrower window; they start below the page.
  • Elite Dangerous: Elite keeps two inputs per action. When two relations put one action on two buttons and the game keeps a keyboard key on it, only one button fits: the export no longer says both were written (the second one used to replace the first). It names the bind that was not written and what holds the two places, and the Check shows it under "Not written to the game" with "Move to another button" and "Keep as is".
  • Elite Dangerous: a hat bind that uses half of an axis as its modifier stays in the game on "Replace on my devices"; the export now says so in plain words.
  • WARDOGS: the export that turns "Enable HOTAS" on no longer also warns that it is off.
  • Maker photos of devices: 62 VKB, Honeycomb and MOZA photos that the shops saved again as other files with exactly the same picture count as the recorded photos again, so JoyPro's marker positions show on them. Comes with the next photo list update.
  • Slaw RF Dora V3: only the maker's photos that clearly show the RF Dora V3 are used (two photos from its own product page); seven pictures from a page that is gone, which did not prove the model, are no longer listed.
  • DCS command lists from the command line: rebuilding the DCS command lists and clean profiles never starts DCS any more unless you ask for it. It now reads DCS's own files in the background by default, as the app does; only --headless-lua false starts DCS and clicks through its menus.
  • Axis curves from the command line: a curve for all games no longer says it stored game-specific values it cannot keep. It shows what it stored and names the values that only work for a game, an aircraft or a game family.
  • The export help of Elite Dangerous and X4 now says that only the ship flight controls are released, ACE COMBAT 8 says that nothing is written, and Hell Let Loose: Vietnam that only the keyboard pilot keys are written (it used to say "only aircraft/helicopter actions" for all four).
  • MSFS 2020: its import help no longer offers an MSFS 2024-only option, and the MSFS 2024-only options given to MSFS 2020 now get a note that they change nothing there.
  • Searching the device catalog from the command line uses every word you type ("vkb gladiator"), not only the first.
  • Importing or scanning MSFS, X-Plane, War Thunder and the other games from the command line with devices to ignore now leaves the binds of those devices out (it was accepted and did nothing; DCS, IL-2 and Star Citizen already did); the result says how many binds were left out.
  • "Replace on my devices" works the same in every game: the devices the export writes get exactly your profile's binds, and your other connected devices keep all of theirs. In X4, Arma 2, Arma 3, Arma Reforger, Battlefield, G.Rebels, War Thunder, Falcon BMS, FSX / Prepar3D, MSFS, X-Plane and VTOL VR it also took your profile's actions off your other devices, as only "Update, my profile wins" should.
  • DCS: "Update, my profile wins" no longer takes your profile's actions off the binds of your other devices that are not plugged in; only connected devices count, as in the other games.
  • IL-2 Korea: when the game had given a device's number to another device too (vJoy and a stick both "joy 5"), the export now lists the exported device under that number, so an import reads its binds back on the right device (they came back on the other stick).
  • Arma Reforger: the game never sees vJoy, so vJoy binds are no longer written (they landed on another device's joystick slot); the export lists them as not written.
  • Import Relations JSON / Import Binds JSON now ask first: the file's relations and binds replace all of the profile's (Undo brings them back). The menu's tooltip said it adds relations; it now says it replaces them.
  • War Thunder: with "read the full command list from the game's archives" switched off in Settings > Games, the game page's Refresh DB and the first scan, import or export of a fresh install respect the switch too (only the Start page's update notice did).
  • From the command line, relations import and binds import no longer silently drop what a partial file (a filtered export, a relations mass export) does not have: such a file is refused with the names of what would go, and --replace imports it anyway and lists what was removed. A --text export or another JSON file gets a clear message ("for reading only", "not a relations file") instead of a crash report.
  • Command line: a word where a number belongs (setup check --modifiers, setup render --width and every other number or true/false option) and an export into a folder that does not exist yet end with a clear message instead of a crash report; the missing folder is created.
  • Command line: dcs strip-defaults --status shows the status (it used to apply the switch), dcs device-luas --status lists the device files, and dcs strip-defaults --remove points to --restore. A failure without an action word names the action.
  • Command line: dcs defaults cut / restore (the older way to move DCS's device files) have --dry-run and are refused while DCS or its updater runs.
  • Command line: relations item add refuses an action its game's list does not have and an axis in a button relation (or the other way round); --force adds it anyway. Editing an action's aircraft in the GUI is not affected.
  • Command line: relations aircraft --node game:id:F-16C_50 finds the action (an aircraft part without | used to match nothing).
  • Command line: setup check ends with exit code 1 when a rule fails, layout callouts when its sheet check finds problems, telemetry recover / prune when a session could not be read or deleted (and prune --text lists the problems).
  • Command line: usage restore takes only a day usage backups lists, usage pin / exclude only a known game and an aircraft of the ranking; export-marks dismiss takes game aliases (il2, wt) and says when nothing matched; devices replace --merge takes the word in any case; media --text and overlay map --text show their warnings; llm disable no longer takes (and silently chooses) a model.
  • Command line: profile history prune --all --dry-run counts the 1 GB step for all profiles too.
  • Command line: Battlefield 6 / 2042 refresh-db --path <folder> reads the profile in that folder, and G-Rebels' refresh-db works without the game (it reads nothing of it). --ignore-devices of MSFS, X-Plane, War Thunder and the other games also takes commas.
  • The command line help and the CLI wiki describe every command (about 100 had a placeholder text), with examples, the real --dry-run meaning, the refresh behaviour per game (rebuilt or added to) and the options that were missing (setup render --drawing-*, War Thunder's archive switch, ffb telemetry config set keys).
  • Cheat sheets are made in every language again: in a language other than English the export stopped after saving the picture's layout. The setup wizard shows your devices and aircraft in every language (they stayed empty).
  • "Skip devices without a picture" also skips a ticked device without one; the others are made.
  • Cockpits page: "Use this layout" opens the setup wizard with that cockpit: its aircraft comes first and the plan places its functions as on that version's stick and throttle. "Close all" in the cockpit guide closes the areas.
  • HUD: "Undo changes" goes back to the style you had before the changes (it went to Glass), and "My screen / The stream" of the preview is kept after a restart.
  • HUD: the page for OBS, the stream window and the other HUD settings could be switched off by themselves when JoyPro started: the HUD's settings were saved over with the defaults before they were read. They are kept now, and Start HUD on the Start page starts the page for OBS as ticked. A HUD settings file that cannot be read is kept next to it (hud.json.unreadable-<time>) instead of being replaced.
  • HUD: a grip's stick element no longer takes its axes from vJoy or from another stick. vJoy (also the device JoyPro tunnels into) never counts as a base, and a base such as the VPforce Rhino keeps its own X and Y: a VKB grip on a Rhino moves with the Rhino, a separate WarBRD stays on its own. The aircraft widget's automatic stick and throttle skip vJoy too.
  • "Manage" next to Your devices opens Settings with the Devices section.
  • Virtual joystick: Test no longer changes the profile (the mapping is only checked; Start saves it).
  • Replace device: "Move binds" asks first and keeps a copy of the profile next to it, like Exchange Device and Remove.
  • Settings > DCS: changing "Keep the UI layer's default binds" while DCS's default binds are switched off makes DCS's input files again with the new choice; the choice is kept after a restart.
  • Kept after a restart: the Layout page's Snap, Grid, Tables, 3D view and Tables and lines, the "Where JoyPro looks" ticks, the pictures picked with Apply Selected, and the cheat-sheet options and output folder.
  • Corrected tooltips (Follow input, Expand with AI, Reset colours, Reset markers, the aircraft check's undo steps, Save as last profile) and the last English texts of the DCS and IL-2 settings, the profile path and the file dialogs translated into every language.
  • Virtual joystick: the Virtual Joystick page now says when to start the virtual Xbox pad of Hell Let Loose: Vietnam and ACE COMBAT 8: these games only see a pad created after their title screen shows, so press Start there (or Stop and Start once the title screen shows). The note is under step 3 of the Overview and in the tooltips of Start and of the auto start, for the game you set to Xbox pad.
  • IL-2: the import no longer picks a different device for one on your ignore list or for the vJoy device JoyPro tunnels into (it could leave out another vJoy device and read the tunnel device). An ignored device named without its number ("vJoy Device") leaves out every device of that name. The same applies to the DCS and Star Citizen imports.
