JoyPro 2.2.13

From docs/releases/v2.2.13.md in the repository (generated, always the current text).

Optional local AI helpers for your relations, and names of your own for your devices.

#New

  • Manage with AI (Relations list): describe what to do - create a relation, delete or rename many relations, add the actions of new aircraft to every relation they fit ("fill in the F-15E, F-4E and CH-47F") - and JoyPro shows a plan to review: a summary, then each relation with the actions to add or remove, where each came from (JoyPro's rules, the AI or both) and why. Tick what you want and apply it as one undo step. Simple requests work without a model.
  • Edit with AI for the open relation: the same, only for that relation, shown as before/after.
  • Local AI status: shows whether the local model runs, which one and how much (video) memory it uses; "Stop now" frees it at once, and it stops by itself after a few idle minutes (Settings, default 2). It also stops when JoyPro closes.
  • Device names of your own: give a device a name on the Layout page (for example to tell two similar panels apart). JoyPro shows it everywhere it shows the device; the real name stays in the tooltip, and Settings > Devices switches between both. Exports always use the real device.

#Fixed

  • A layout suggestion no longer stores the drawing's name as the device's name.
