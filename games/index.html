Games

JoyPro handles 36 game titles and has notes on 21 more. Each page below explains one game (or one family of games that share their files) in three levels, like the GUI pages: Beginner (import, relations, export and what you then see in the game), Advanced (the files, what each export mode does in that game, what is never touched, curves, force feedback, the watcher) and Technical (file formats, how devices are identified, the engine commands). The Show: switch at the top of a game page picks the level.

▶
Video: JoyPro and your games: one profile, every game
Coming later: one YouTube video per topic, recorded with the English interface, with subtitles in every language JoyPro speaks.

Every game follows the same rules: JoyPro writes only the actions of the game's command list, refuses to write while the game runs, backs the files up first (Undo last export on the Export page puts them back byte for byte) and skips a device or action it cannot match instead of guessing. Devices that are not connected, your mouse, the import's ignored devices and the vJoy device JoyPro tunnels into are never cleared.
