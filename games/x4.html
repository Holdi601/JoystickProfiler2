X4: Foundations

Show:

X4: Foundations keeps your binds in inputmap.xml and names a joystick by its slot (1 to 8), not by its name. Which device sits in which slot is in config.xml. JoyPro reads the space flight binds into your profile and writes them back in place, to the slots X4 has pinned to your devices. Inverts and the dead zone are game-wide in X4, so JoyPro sets them from your relations for the whole game.

For beginners

#What JoyPro does for X4

  • Import: the space flight binds of your sticks, throttles and pedals come into your profile, with the game's inverts and throttle mode.
  • Relations: one relation is one function in every game. "Boost" can be the same button in X4, Elite Dangerous and Star Citizen.
  • Export: JoyPro edits inputmap.xml in place, on the slots of your devices. Menu, first-person, keyboard and mouse binds stay.

#Import your X4 binds

  1. Open Start. If the X4 card asks for a command list, press Build List: JoyPro reads the game's controls from your installation (read only).
  2. Press Import on the X4 card. Keep Add to my profile. Tick Also take the keyboard keys if you want your flight keys in JoyPro too.
  3. Press Import now. See my relations opens what came in.

#Build and check relations

Join the same function of your games with the relations flow, then press Check on the Export page. Keep your joystick binds without modifiers: X4 takes no modifier on a joystick bind.

#Export to X4

  1. Close X4. The game writes inputmap.xml and config.xml when a session ends, so JoyPro refuses to write while it runs.
  2. Open Export and tick the X4 tile.
  3. Keep Replace on my devices with Also clear my other devices if JoyPro is where you set up your sticks. Press Export now on the tile.
In the game: What you see in X4

Start the game and open Settings > Controls. Your binds are in the controller / joystick column of each space flight control, on your device's slot. Invert Controller / Joystick Axes shows the inverts JoyPro set, and the bidirectional throttle, joystick dead zone and adaptive steering rows show your values. Auto-roll and rumble are in the game settings.

#What you do yourself in X4

  • Give every device a slot once. In X4's controls settings, open Manage Controllers/Joysticks and assign each stick, throttle and pedal set to a slot, then leave the game normally. X4 then remembers the device in config.xml. A device without such an entry moves between slots from one session to the next, so JoyPro skips it ("assign the device to a slot in X4 once"). A device set to "none" there is skipped too.
  • Plug your devices in before you export.

#Common pitfalls

  • "X4 is running": exit the game and export again.
  • A device was skipped: it is not pinned to a slot in X4. Assign it under Manage Controllers/Joysticks.
  • A stick in slot 1 does gamepad things: slot 1 is the gamepad slot of X4's default map, so a stick there inherits the stock gamepad binds. Replace on my devices removes them.
  • Buttons above 64 or a second hat do nothing: X4 reads 64 buttons, 8 axes and one hat per device. JoyPro skips binds beyond that with a warning.
  • An invert was left alone: one invert counts for every device on that control. When your relations disagree, or a gamepad is bound to the same control, JoyPro leaves the game's value and warns.
Advanced

#Files JoyPro reads and writes

File What it is
%USERPROFILE%\Documents\Egosoft\X4\<id>\inputmap.xml the active binds (<action>, <state>, <range> per control and input) and the <config> params (inverts, dead zone, bidirectional throttle, adaptive steering). <id> is the Steam account's folder.
...\X4\<id>\config.xml the device slots (<joysticks>) and game settings such as rumble, auto-roll and gamepad mode; read for the slots, edited in place for the settings
...\X4\<id>\inputmap.xml.joypro-meta.json JoyPro's names, groups and tuning of the written binds, read back by the next import
inputmap.xml.jpbak, config.xml.jpbak copies from before the first write, plus JoyPro's versioned backup store

The file is written as text: the declaration, line endings, indentation, the section comments, <config>, the root attributes and every untouched line stay byte for byte. A new line goes into its section after the last entry of the same kind with a lower control number. JoyPro never touches the saved profiles inputmap_1.xml to inputmap_5.xml or your saves. Steam Cloud syncs the folder; which files it syncs is not confirmed yet.

#The export modes in X4

A conflict is another flight control on the same slot and input. The map and detail-monitor twins of a control count as the same control.

Mode What happens in inputmap.xml
Only add Nothing is removed. A bind whose input another flight control has is not written and gets a mark "Not written to the game".
Update, keep the game's binds Your lines go in beside the control's other inputs; only conflicts are removed.
Update, my profile wins As above, and a control you bind loses its other lines on pinned slots of connected devices (with the keyboard option also its keyboard lines).
Replace on my devices The flight binds of the written slots that are not in your profile are removed, also the stock gamepad flight binds on slot 1.
+ Also clear my other devices The same for slots that config.xml pins to connected devices the export does not use. A slot without a pinned device is left alone: its device is unknown.

X4's menu binds some controls together: "Toggle Travel Mode" sets the same input inside the map too, "Dock" also undocks. JoyPro writes and removes such groups as the menu does, and never removes the first-person "Interact" twin.

#What is never touched

  • Menu, map, first-person, options and accessibility controls; radial menu entries.
  • Keyboard and mouse lines; the keyboard is written only with the keyboard option, only for flight controls, never with the keys X4's menu refuses (Escape, Delete, Ctrl+X/C/V ...).
  • Slots of devices that are not connected or not pinned, gamepads, the import's ignored devices and the vJoy device JoyPro tunnels into.
  • Every <config> line JoyPro does not set.

#Curves and game settings

  • Granularity: one set of binds for every ship (the space flight controls).
  • Inverts are one value per control for every device (invert_steering_yaw, _pitch, _roll, invert_throttle, invert_strafe_*). Your relation keeps DCS's direction; X4's own HOTAS maps count pitch, roll and throttle as inverted, so JoyPro sets the param to your invert turned over for those. It sets a param only when every JoyPro axis on that control agrees.
  • Bidirectional throttle follows the throttle relation's Slider: Slider = forward only, no Slider = centre is zero thrust.
  • Dead zone: X4 has one dead zone for every axis of every joystick. Relation dead zones are not written (the export says so); set the game's dead zone in Settings > Force feedback settings of the games (pick X4) or with engine ffb set. The same place holds adaptive steering, rumble, auto-roll and gamepad mode. A setting you store there wins over the relations, with a warning when it reverses them.
  • No per-axis curves or saturation in X4; no force feedback; no telemetry.
  • Watcher: a bind or option you change in the game is noticed after it closes (inputmap.xml and config.xml), and JoyPro asks whether to take it over.
  • HUD: the HUD finds the game by its process and shows your X4 binds.

#X Rebirth and X3

X Rebirth uses the same inputmap.xml words as X4, but its command list would need its own extraction from an installation. X3 (Reunion, Terran Conflict, Albion Prelude) has another engine and an unknown file format. Both are documented only: JoyPro does not import or export them.

Technical internals

#Inside the X4 integration

Code: engine\Games\X4Integration.cs, engine\Games\X4CatalogSource.cs and the settings model engine\FfbModels\X4.cs.

  • Slots: a bind names a source: INPUT_SOURCE_JOYBUTTONS (buttons and hat) or INPUT_SOURCE_JOYAXES, with no suffix for slot 1 and _2 to _8 for the others. config.xml maps devices to slots by DirectInput instance GUID, written in X4's own form ({42cec570-0a3f-11f0-80-0b-44-45-53-54-00-00}); num 0 to 7 is the slot, disabled="1" means none. JoyPro writes only to slots whose current, enabled entry matches a connected device.
  • Codes: buttons 1 to 11 use X4's gamepad names (INPUT_XBUTTON_A ... BIGBUTTON), 12 to 64 INPUT_XBUTTON_12 ...; the hat INPUT_XBUTTON_DPAD_* (eight directions); axes INPUT_JOYAXIS_X ... RZ, SLIDER1, SLIDER2. Ranges get no sgn. Keyboard: INPUT_KEYCODE_<DIK> with _SHIFT / _CONTROL.
  • Command list: engine x4 refresh-db reads gameoptions.lua and the English text page from the .cat/.dat archives and the name tables of X4.exe, read only; 128 space flight controls (6 axes, 122 buttons). Rows read as "Yaw (Primary Steering Axis)", "Roll (Secondary Steering Axis)". engine x4 catalog lists it, engine x4 paths shows the files found.
  • Import / export: engine x4 import, engine x4 scan; engine x4 export --mode clean|add|merge|merge-overwrite --nuke-untouched-devices --include-keyboard --ignore-device <d> --dry-run.
  • Game settings: engine ffb set --device * --game X4 --param <key>=<value>; params are edited in place, a missing one is added as the last line of <config>. An export without X4 binds still writes the stored settings.

#Known limits

  • Devices without a pinned slot are skipped; JoyPro does not pin them itself yet.
  • Joystick modifiers are not written: their markup in the file is not confirmed yet.
  • 64 buttons, 8 axes and one hat per device; eight slots, shared with gamepads.
  • Rumble: the game's slider shows the value times 100 cut off (21 % shows as 20); the file keeps the exact value.
  • Tests: engine\tests\smoke\x4.ps1 (also the catalog refresh); in the game tools\e2e\games\X4.ps1 (binds and mode matrix, in its own profile folder) and tools\e2e\games\X4Settings.ps1 (inverts, throttle mode, game settings).
